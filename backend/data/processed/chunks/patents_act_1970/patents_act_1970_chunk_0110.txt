118. Alteration of names etc. in the register of patent agents. - (1) A patent agent may apply for the alteration of his name, address of the principal place of business and branch offices, if any, or the qualifications entered in the register of patent agents. On receipt of such application and the fee specified therefor in the First Schedule, the Controller shall cause the necessary alterations to be made in the register of patent agents. 
(2) Every alteration made in the register of patent agents shall be notified in the Official 
Gazette.
