52. Procedure to be followed in case of application under rule 51. - (1) If the Controller allows the application to be proceeded with, he shall direct the applicant to serve copies of the application and of the evidence in support thereof upon the holder of exclusive marketing rights or upon any other person on whom, in his opinion, such copies should be served. 
(2) The applicant shall inform the Controller the date on which the service of copies of application and of the evidence on the holder of exclusive marketing right and other persons referred to in sub-rule (I) has effected.
