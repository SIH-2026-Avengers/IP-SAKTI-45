57. Filing of written statement of opposition and evidence. - The opponent shall send a written statement in duplicate setting out the nature of the opponent's interest, the facts upon which he bases his case and relief which he seeks and evidence, if any, along with notice of opposition or within two months from the date of the notice of opposition and shall deliver to the applicant a copy of the statement and the evidence.
