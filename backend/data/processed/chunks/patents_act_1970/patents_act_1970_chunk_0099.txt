CHAPTER XIV 
SCIENTIFIC ADVISERS 
103. Ro11 of scientific advisers. - (I) The Controller shall maintain a roll of scientific advisers for the purpose of section 115. The roll shall be updated annually. The roll shall contain the names and addresses of scientific advisers, their designations, information regarding their educational qualifications, the disciplines of their specialisation and their technical, practical and research experience.
