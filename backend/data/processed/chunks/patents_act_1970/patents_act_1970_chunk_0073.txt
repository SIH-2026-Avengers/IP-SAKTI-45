CHAPTER VIII 
SEALING OF PATENTS 
73. Sealing of patents. - (I) A request for the sealing of a patent under sub-section (I) of section 43 shall be made in Form 9. 
(2) The period within which a request for the sealing of a patent may be made under clause 
(a) of the proviso to sub-section (2) of section 43 shall be two months after the final determination to the proceeding referred to in that clause. 
(3) An application under sub-section (3) ofsection 43 shall be made in Form 4.
