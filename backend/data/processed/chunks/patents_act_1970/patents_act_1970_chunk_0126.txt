138. Power to extend time prescribed. - (1) The time prescnbed by these rules for doing of any act or the taking of any proceeding thereunder may be extended by the Controller normally not more than three months, if he thinks it fit to do so and upon such terms as he may direct: 
Provided that only one such extension shall be granted by the Controller in every matter before him. 
(2) Any request for extension of time made under these rules shall be made within the extendable period by request unless otherwise provided in these rules.
