79. Request under secdon 52 (2). - (I) A request under sub-section (2) of section 52 shall be made in Fonn 12 within three months from the date of the order of the court referred to in sub~section (1) of the said section and shall be accompanied by a statement setting out the facts upon which the petitioner relies and the relief he claims and a certified copy ofthe order of the court. 
(2) Where the court has ordered the grant of patent to the applicant only for a part of the invention, the new patent granted shall be accorded a number in the same series of numbers accorded to the complete specifications accepted on the same day as the patent is granted.
