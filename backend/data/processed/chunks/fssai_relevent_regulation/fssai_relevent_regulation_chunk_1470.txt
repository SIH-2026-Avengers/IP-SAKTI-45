1.4.2 Sterilized and UHT creams, whipping and whipped creams, and reduced fat creams 
(plain) 
Includes every cream, regardless of fat content, which has undergone a higher heat-treatment than pasteurization, pasteurized creams with a reduced fat content, as well as every cream intended for whipping or being whipped. Sterilized cream is subjected to appropriate heat-treatment in the container in which it is presented to the consumer. Ultra-heat treated (UHT) or ultra-pasteurized cream is subjected to the appropriate heat treatment (UHT or ultra-pasteurization) in a continuous flow process and aseptically packaged. Cream may also be packaged under pressure (whipped cream). Includes whipping cream, heavy cream, whipped pasteurized cream, and whipped cream- type dairy toppings and fillings. Creams or toppings with partial or total replacement of milk fat by other fats are included in sub-category 1.4.4 (cream analogues).
