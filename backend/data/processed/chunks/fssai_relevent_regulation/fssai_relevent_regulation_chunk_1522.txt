6.2.1 Flour 
Flour is produced from the milling of grain, cereals and tubers (e.g. cassava) and seeds, pith or soft core of palm tree. Includes flour pastes for bread and flour confectionery, flour for bread, pastries, noodles and pasta, and flour mixes (physical mixtures of flours from different cereal or grain sources, which are different from mixes for bakery goods (dry mixes containing flour and other ingredients, categories 7.1.6 (mixes for ordinary bakery wares) and 7.2.3 (mixes for fine bakery wares) such as 
Atta, besan, suji, durum wheat flour, self-rising flour, enriched flour, instantized flour, corn flour, corn meal, kuttu-ka-atta, singhade-ka-atta, roasted soybean flour,konjac flour, and maida (refined wheat flour) and sago flour.
