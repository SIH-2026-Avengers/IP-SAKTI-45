2.9.4: Cinnamon (Dalchini) 
 
1. Cinnamon (Dalchini) whole means the inner bark of trunks or branches of Cinnamomum 
Zeylanicum Blume. It shall have characteristic odour and flavour and shall be free from foreign flavour and mustiness. It shall be free from mould, living and dead insects, insect fragments, rodent contamination. The product shall be free from added colouring matter, foreign vegetable matter and other harmful substances. 
 
It shall conform to the following standards: 
 
(i) 
Extraneous matter 
Not more than 1.0 percent by weight
