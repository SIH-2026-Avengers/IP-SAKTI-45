77[2.9.42 Dried Sweet Basil Leaves: 
1. Dried sweet basil leaves are obtained from the species Ocimumbasilicum L., belonging to the family Lamiaceae. Dried sweet basil leaves shall have characteristic colour, odour and flavour. 
It shall be free from visible moulds, living/dead insects, insect fragments and rodent contamination. The product shall be free from foreign odours, flavours, any other harmful substances and added colouring matters. It shall conform to the following requirements:
