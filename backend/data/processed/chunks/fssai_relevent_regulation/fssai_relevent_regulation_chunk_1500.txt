4.2.2 Processed vegetables (including mushrooms and fungi, roots and tubers, pulses and legumes, and aloe vera), seaweeds, and nuts and seeds 
Includes all forms of processing other than peeling, cutting and surface treating of fresh vegetables. 
 
4.2.2.1 Frozen vegetables (including mushrooms and fungi, roots and tubers, pulses and legumes, and aloe vera), seaweeds, and nuts and seeds 
Fresh vegetables are usually blanched and frozen. Examples include quick-frozen corn, quick-frozen 
French-fried potatoes, quick frozen peas, and quick frozen whole processed tomatoes.
