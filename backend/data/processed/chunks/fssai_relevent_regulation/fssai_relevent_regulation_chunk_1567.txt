12.1.2 Salt substitutes 
Salt substitutes are seasonings with reduced sodium content intended to be used on food in place of salt. 
 
52[12.2 Herbs, spices, seasonings, and condiments 
This category describes items intended to enhance the aroma and taste of food. Spices means any form of spice including curry powders, spice oils, oleoresins and other mixtures where spice content is predominant.]
