53[4. Formulated supplements for children 
(1) Scope: This standard specifies requirements of formulated supplements for children of age above 24 months till 36 months. 
 
(2) Description: Formulated supplements for children shall be of appropriate nutritional quality to provide additional energy and nutrients to complement the family foods derived from the local produce by providing those nutrients that are either lacking or are present in insufficient quantities. 
These foods may be presented in any other age suitable food format.
