5.0 Confectionery 
Includes all cocoa and chocolate products (5.1), other confectionery products that may or may not contain cocoa (5.2), chewing gum (5.3), and decorations and icings (5.4), or foods produced solely with any combination of foods conforming to these sub-categories.
