14.1.4.3 Concentrates (liquid or solid) for water-based flavoured drinks 
Include powder, syrup, liquid and frozen concentrates for the preparation of carbonated or non- carbonated water-based non-alcoholic beverages by addition of water or carbonated water. Examples include squashes, fountain syrups (e.g. cola syrup), fruit syrups for soft drinks, frozen or powdered concentrate for lemonade and iced tea mixes.
