All animal fats and oils should be derived from animals in good health at the time of slaughter and intended for human consumption. 
 
2.2 Fat emulsions mainly of type water-in-oil 
Include all emulsified products excluding fat-based counterparts of dairy products and dairy desserts. 
 
2.2.1 Butter 
Butter is a fatty product consisting of a primarily water-in-oil emulsion derived exclusively from milk or products obtained from milk or both. 
 
2.2.2 Fat spreads, dairy fat spreads and blended spreads
