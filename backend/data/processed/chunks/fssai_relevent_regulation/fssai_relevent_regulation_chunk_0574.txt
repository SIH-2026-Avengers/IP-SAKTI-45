6. Labelling: 
The product covered by this standard shall be labelled in accordance with the Food Safety and 
Standards (Packaging and Labelling) Regulations, 2011 and in addition, the name of style of product shall be labelled on the product. 
7. Method of analysis: 
The product shall be analysed as provided in the relevant Food Safety and Standards Authority of India Manual of Method of Analysis of Food.
