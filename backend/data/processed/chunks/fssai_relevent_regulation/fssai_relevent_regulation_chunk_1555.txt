9.4 Fully preserved, including canned or fermented fish and fish products, including molluscs, crustaceans, and echinoderms 
Products with extended shelf life, manufactured by pasteurizing or steam retorting and packaging in vacuumsealedair-tight containers to ensure sterility. Products may be packed in their own juice or in added oil or sauce. This category excludes fully cooked products (see category 9.2.4) such ascanned tuna, clams, crab, fish roe and sardines; gefilte fish balls; and surimi (heat-pasteurized).
