Natural mineral water may be naturally carbonated (with carbon dioxide from the source), carbonated 
(with added carbon dioxide of another origin), decarbonised (with less carbon dioxide than present in the water at the source so it does not spontaneously give off carbon dioxide under conditions of standard temperature and pressure), or fortified (with carbon dioxide from the source), and non- carbonated (contains no free carbon dioxide).
