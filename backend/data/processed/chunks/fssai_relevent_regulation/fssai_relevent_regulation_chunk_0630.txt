2.4.6 
Food grains: 
 
1. 
Food grains meant for human consumption shall be whole or broken kernels of cereals, millets and pulses. In addition to the undermentioned standards to which foodgrains shall conform, they shall be free from Argemone, Maxicana and Kesari in any form. They shall be free from added colouring matter. The foodgrains shall not contain any insecticide residues other than those specified in regulation 2.3.1 of Food Safety and Standards (Contaminants, Toxins and Residues) Regulation, 
2011 and the amount of insecticide residue in the foodgrains shall not exceed the limits specified in 
Regulation 2.3.1. of the said Table Food Safety and standards (Contaminants, Toxins and Residues) 
Regulation, 2011. The foodgrains meant for grinding/processing shall be clean, free from all impurities including foreign matter (extraneous matter).
