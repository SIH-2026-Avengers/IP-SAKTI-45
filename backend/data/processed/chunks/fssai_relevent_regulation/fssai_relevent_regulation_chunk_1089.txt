2.9.11: 56[Dried Ginger (Sonth, Dried Adrak) 
 
1. Dried Ginger (Sonth, Dried Adrak)] whole means the dried rhizome of Zingiber officinale 
Roscoe in pieces irregular in shape and size, pale brown in colour with peel not entirely removed and washed and dried in sun. It may be bleached with lime. It shall have characteristic taste and flavour
