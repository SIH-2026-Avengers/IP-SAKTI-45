2.3.61 Canned Chestnuts and Canned Chestnut Puree 
1. Description 
(a)Canned chestnuts is the product (i) prepared from fresh, sound, mature chestnuts of varieties conforming to the characteristics of the species Castaneacrenata Sieb et Zucc. (Japanese chestnut) or Castanea sativa Miller (European chestnut) which shall be shelled and may be pellicled or unpellicled; (ii) packed with or without water which may or may not contain sugars, seasonings and other ingredients appropriate to the product; and (iii) processed by heat in an appropriate manner, before or after being hermetically sealed in a container, so as to prevent spoilage. 
It may contain "salt" (sodium chloride) in an amount not exceeding 1 percent of total net contents.
