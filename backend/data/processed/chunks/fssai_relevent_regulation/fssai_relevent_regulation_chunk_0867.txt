3. Egg powder 
(1) The standard specified in this clause apply to 'Egg powder' obtained under hygienic conditions from the liquid contents of sound, wholesome, hens' (Gallus gallus) eggs by suitable drying. The product so obtained shall retain the original properties of fresh eggs, like solubility of protein, aerating capacity, binding power and palatability. 
(2) For the purpose of this clause,- 
(a) Whole Egg powder: Product prepared from suitable drying of whole egg liquid with maximum permissible moisture content of 2.0 % and free from any extraneous material and off odour;
