2.3.45 
Grated Desiccated Coconut: 
 
1. 
Grated Desiccated Coconut means the product obtained by peeling, milling and drying the kernel of coconut (cocos nucifera). The product may be in the form of thin flakes, chips or shreds. 
The product shall be white in colour free from foreign matter, living insects, mould, dead insects, insect fragments and rodent contamination. The product shall have pleasant taste and flavour, free from rancidity and evidence of fermentation. The product may contain food additives permitted in these regulations including Appendix A.
