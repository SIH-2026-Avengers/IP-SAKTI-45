"Malai" means the product rich in milk fat prepared by boiling and cooling of cow milk, buffalo milk or milk of any other species as defined under this regulation or a mixture thereof. It is characterized by presence of insoluble mass, principally fat and denatured protein, formed on heating and cooling of milk.
