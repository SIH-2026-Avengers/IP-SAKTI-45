67[2.3.38.A FROZEN BEANS 
1. Frozen Beans shall be prepared from fresh, clean, sound, succulent pods of the plants of the species 
Phaseolus vulgaris L. or Phaseoluscoccineus L. Strings, stems, and stem ends shall be removed, and the pods are washed and sufficiently blanched to ensure inactivation of enzymes and adequate stability of colour and flavour and shall be stored at -18°C or below.
