2.9.3: Chillies and Capsicum (Lal Mirchi) 
 
1. Chillies and Capsicum (Lal Mirchi) whole - means the dried ripe fruits or pods of the 
Capsicum annum L & Capsicum frutescens L. The pods shall be free from mould, living and dead insects, insect fragments, rodent contamination. The product shall be free from extraneous colouring matter, coating of mineral oil and other harmful substances. 
It shall conform to the following standards:- 
(i) 
Extraneous matter 
Not more than 1.0 percent by weight 
(ii) Unripe and marked fruits 
Not more than 2.0 percent by weight 
(iii) Broken fruits, seed & fragments 
Not more than 5.0 percent by weight 
(iv) Moisture
