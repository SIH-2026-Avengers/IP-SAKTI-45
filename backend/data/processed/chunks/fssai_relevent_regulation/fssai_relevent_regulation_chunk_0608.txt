 (vi) Quick Frozen Fungi means fresh edible fungi of one species which, after cleaning, washing and blanching, are subjected to a freezing process in appropriate equipment. This freezing operation shall be carried out in such a way that the range of temperature of maximum crystallization is passed quickly. The quick-freezing process shall not be regarded as complete unless and until the product temperature has reached -18oC (0oF) at the thermal centre after thermal stabilization.
