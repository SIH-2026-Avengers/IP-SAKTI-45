2.1.22 
Whey Protein Concentrate: This Standard applies to Whey Protein Concentrate as defined in item 1 of this sub-regulation.  
1.Description.- 

Whey protein concentrate means a product obtained by removing non-protein constituents from whey by means of physical separation techniques such as precipitation, filtration, dialysis and other relevant techniques, followed by drying.
