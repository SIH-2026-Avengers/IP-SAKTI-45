I.FOOD CATEGORY SYSTEM 
 
The food category system is a tool for assigning food additive uses in these Regulations. The food category system applies to all foodstuffs. The food category descriptors are not to be legal product designations nor are they intended for labelling purposes. The food category system is based on the following principles: 
 
(a) The food category system is hierarchical, meaning that when an additive is recognised for use in a general category, it is recognised for use in all its sub-categories, unless otherwise stated. Similarly, when an additive is recognised for use in a sub-category, its use is recognised in any further subcategories or individual foodstuffs mentioned in a sub-category. The food category system is based on product descriptors of foodstuffs as marketed, unless otherwise stated.
