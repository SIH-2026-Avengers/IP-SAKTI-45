72[2. Frozen Egg Products. - (1) The standard specified in this clause shall apply to frozen egg products designated as "Frozen egg white or albumen", "frozen egg yolk" and "frozen whole egg" prepared from hens' (Gallus gallus) eggs packaged in any suitable packaging material. 
(2) for the purpose of this clause, - 
(a) Frozen egg productmeans the whole egg, egg yolk, or white whichis pasteurized and frozen; 
(b) Whole eggmeans the homogeneous product obtained from the complete contents of broken out hens eggs-in-shell; 
(c) Egg yolk meansthe homogeneous product produced from the separation of the yolk of broken out hens -eggs-in-shell;
