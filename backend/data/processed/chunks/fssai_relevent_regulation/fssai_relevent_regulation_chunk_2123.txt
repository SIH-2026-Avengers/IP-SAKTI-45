 Maximum usage level shall not be more than 200 mg/kg for wine, 250 mg/kg for fruits and vegetable juices and its products and 250 mg/kg for water based flavoured drinks. Residue shall be analyzed as per method specified in "Joint FAO/WHO Expert Committee on Food Additives 
(JECFA) specification of Dimethyl dicarbonate".
