2.13.4: Record of Radiation Processing of Food - 
 
Any treatment of food by radiation shall be recorded by facility as specified under the 
Atomic Energy (Radiation Processing of Food and Allied Products) Rules, 2012. 
 
2.13.5: Standards of Radiation Processed Food - 
 
The Radiation processed food shall comply with all the provisions of the Food Safety and 
Standards Act and the Regulations made thereunder specifying standards of such food. 
2.13.6: Storage and Sale of Radiation Processed Food - 
 
Save as otherwise provided in these regulations, no person shall irradiate for sale, store for sale, or transport for sale irradiated food. 
2.13.7: Restriction on Sale of Radiation Processed Food - 
 
The Radiation processed food shall be offered for sale only in pre-packaged conditions.
