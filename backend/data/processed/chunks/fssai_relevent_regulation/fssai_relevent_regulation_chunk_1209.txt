(A) 
Any additional item or ingredient proposed to be added (other than the substances prescribed in the said table will be subject to approval by the Food Authority after safety assessment and substantiating scientific evidence. 
(i) 
A declaration "consume not more than 500 ml per day" shall be made on the label that represents the per day quantity. 
(ii) 
The per day quantity is the maximum amount of caffeinated beverage (as package or serves) that is consumed in one day and determined as in sub-clause (iii). 
(iii) 
Where any one of the substances given in column (1) of the following table is consumed at the maximum level given in column (2), it shall represent the per day quantity and the declaration shall be made on the basis of the number of packs of serves that cumulatively delivers this amount when consumed in a day.
