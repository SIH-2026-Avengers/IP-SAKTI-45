2. Food Additives 
Only those food additives permitted under the Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011 shall be used. 
3. Hygiene 
The product shall be prepared and handled in accordance with the guideline provided in Schedule 4, 
Part-II of the Food Safety and Standards (Licensing and Registration of Food Businesses) Regulation, 
2011 and such guidance as provided from time to time under the provisions of the Food Safety and 
Standards Act, 2006.
