3.3.5 Phyto or Plant Stanol 
66[Phyto or Plant stanol esters may be added to the following products so as to allow users to easily restrict their consumption to maximum 3 g stanol per day through the use of either one portion containing maximum 3 g or three portions each containing 1 g and it shall be added subject to the table declaration under sub-regulation 48 of regulation 2.4.5 of the Food Safety and Standards 
(Packaging and Labelling) Regulations, 2011, namely:-] 
(i) 
Fat spread, milk products, milk based fruit drink, fermented milk products, soy and rice drink, cheese products, yoghurt products, spice sauces, salad dressings, juices and nectars.
