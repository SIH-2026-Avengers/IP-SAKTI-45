The product shall be free from foreign odours, flavours and free from any other harmful substances and added colours. 
(a) Black pepper -It shall be dried berries having unbroken pericarp The product shall be whole with globular shape and wrinkled pericarp and shall have diameter of minimum 2.0 mm. It shall be brownish to dark brownish or blackish in colour. The flavours shall have a penetrating odour and hot, biting pungent taste characteristics of black pepper excluding mouldy and rancid odours. 
(b) White pepper - It shall be dried berries after removing the pericarp. The product shall be whole with globular shape with smooth surface, slightly flattened at one pole and a small protuberance at the other and shall have diameter of minimum 1.8 mm. It shall be matt grey to brownish to pale ivory
