66[2.3.63. COCONUT MILK POWDER. - (1) "Coconut milk powder" means dehydrated or spray dried product obtained by removal of water from the coconut milk obtained from fresh, wholesome kernels of the fruits of coconut palm (CocosnuciferaL.), maltodextrin and sodium caseinate may be added to the product. 
(2) It shall have flavour and odour characteristic of the products and shall be smooth and free flowing in texture and creamish to white or off white in colour. 
(3) The product shall be free from added colouring or flavouring matter.
