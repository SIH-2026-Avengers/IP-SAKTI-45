5.2.2 Soft candy 
Products include soft, chewy products such as caramels (containing sugar syrup, fats, colour and flavour) and their dietetic counterparts; products that may or may not contain cocoa and milk (e.g. toffees and chocolate-flavoured caramels); jelly-based candies (e.g. jelly beans, jellied fruit paste covered in sugar, made from pectin, colour and flavour); and licorice. Also included are halwa, and oriental specialties, such as sweet bean jelly etc. These types of products may be used as fillings for chocolate products within the scope of food categories 5.1.3 and 5.1.4.
