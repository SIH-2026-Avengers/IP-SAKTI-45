Oligofructose may be added at not more than 10 per cent of the product, in the following products, subject to label declaration under sub-regulation 43 of regulation 2.4.5 of the Food Safety and 
Standards (Packaging and Labelling) Regulations, 2011, - 
Dairy products like yoghurt, mousse, spreads, dairy based drinks (milkshakes, yoghurt drink), cheese, pudding, cream and ice-cream, frozen desserts like non dairy ice, sorbet and fruit ice, frozen yoghurt, flakes and ready-to-eat dry breakfast cereals, chocolate and sweets and carbohydrate based and milk product based sweets like halwa, mysore pak, boondi laddu, jalebi, khoyaburfi, peda, gulabjamun, rasgulla and similar milk product based sweets sold by any name; cooked sausages, ham and meat spreads. 
73[3.3.4 Trehalose. - (1) Trehalose shall be in the form of white or almost white crystals; soluble in water, slightly soluble in ethanol.
