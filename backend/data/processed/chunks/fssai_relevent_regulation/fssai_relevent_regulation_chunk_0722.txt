1. (A) Rolled Oats. - Rolled/Flaked Oats (Quick Cooking Oats) means the product made from sound hulled oats(AvenaSativa).It shall be free from added colours, rancidity and flavoring agents. 
It shall be in form of flakes of uniform size having a light cream colour. 
1. (B) Products containing oats. - (i) Means the product containing oats as the major ingredient including oat bran, oat flour, oat meal, oat-trim, kilned dehulled oats, Steel cut Oats, Oat flakes 
(various thickness, which may make them instant, quick cooking or whole oats) etc. These may be made by using various process such as kilning, drying, rolling, slitting, cutting, steel cutting, gritting, de-hulling, flaking, grinding etc.
