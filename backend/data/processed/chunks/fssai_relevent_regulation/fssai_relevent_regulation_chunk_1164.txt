2.9.37 Dried Laurel or Dried Bay Leaf - 
(1) Dried Laurel or Dried Bay Leaf Whole means the dried leaf of the tree Laurus nobilis L. The laurel leaf is oblong and pointed at the tip with a short petiole. The leaf is soft, shiny on the surface and dull underneath. It shall have pleasant, strong and delicate odour which emanates strongly when the leaf is crushed. It shall have characteristic flavour.
