Alumina used in colour shall conform to following, namely:- 
(a) 
Identity: Alumina (dried as aluminium hydroxide) is a white, odourless, tasteless, amorphous powder consisting essentially of Aluminium hydroxide (Al2O3 × H2O). 
(b) 
Specifications: Alumina (dried aluminium hydroxide) shall conform to the following specifications, namely:- 
(i) 
Acidity or alkalinity 
Agitate 1 gm with 25ml of water and filter. 
 
The filtrate shall be neutral to litmus paper 
(ii) 
Lead (as Pb) not more than 10 parts per million 
(iii) 
Arsenic (as As) not more than 1 parts per million 
(iv) 
Mercury (as Hg) not more than 1 parts per million 
(v) 
Aluminium oxide (Al2O3) not less than 50 percent 
Solubility: Lakes are insoluble in most solvents. They are also insoluble in water in pH range from 3.5-9.0 but outside this range and lake substrate tends to dissolve releasing the captive dye.
