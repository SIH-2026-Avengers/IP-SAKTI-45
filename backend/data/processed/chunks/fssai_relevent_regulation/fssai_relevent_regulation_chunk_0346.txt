8. Mango Kernel Fat means the fat obtained from clean and sound kernels of Mango (Magifera 
Indica Linn) by process of expression or by a process of solvent extraction from cake or kernel. It shall be refined. The fat shall be clear on melting and free from rancidity, adulterants, sediment suspended or other foreign matter, separated water, added colouring and flavouring matters and mineral oil. However, it may contain food additives permitted in these Regulations and Appendices. 
It shall also conform to the following standards, namely :- 
 
(a) 
Butyro-refractometer reading at 40oC, 
43.7-51.6 
 or Refractive Index at 40oC 
1.4550 to 1.4604 
(b) Saponification value 
185-198 
(c) 
Unsaponifiable matters 
Not more than 1.5 per cent by weight
