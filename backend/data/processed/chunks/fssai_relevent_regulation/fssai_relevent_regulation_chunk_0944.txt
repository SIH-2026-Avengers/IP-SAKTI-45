57[ 18. Live and Raw Bivalve Molluscs: 
 Standard specified in this clause shall apply to live bivalve molluscs and to raw bivalve molluscs that have been shucked or frozen or processed to reduce or to limit target organisms while essentially retaining the sensory characteristics of live bivalve molluscs.
