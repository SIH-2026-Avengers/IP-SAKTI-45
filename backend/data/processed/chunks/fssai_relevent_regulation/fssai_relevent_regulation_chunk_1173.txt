75[2.9.41 DRIED SAGE. - (1) Dried sage in form of whole or cut leaves means the leaves of the species Salvia officinalis L., belonging to the family Lamiaceae, collected before flowering then dried. Dried sage shall have a typical, strong and aromatic odour. It shall have characteristic flavour which is very aromatic, pleasant, fresh and slightly bitter.
