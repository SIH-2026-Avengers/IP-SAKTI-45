1.3 
Condensed milk and analogues (plain) 
Includes plain and sweetened types of condensed milk, evaporated milk, and their analogues 
(including beverage whiteners) and products based on skim, part-skim, low-fat and whole milk, blends of evaporated skimmed milk and vegetable fat, and blends of sweetened condensed skimmed milk and vegetable fat. 
 
1.3.1 Condensed milk (plain) 
Condensed milk is obtained by partial removal of water from milk to which sugar may have been added. For evaporated milk, the water removal may be accomplished by heating. Includes partially dehydrated milk, evaporated milk, sweetened condensed milk, and khoya (cow or buffalo milk concentrated by boiling).
