75[2.9.15 BLACK, WHITE & GREEN (BWG) PEPPERS. - (1) Black, White & Green (BWG) peppers are the berries of Piper nigrumL. of the Piperaceae family having reached appropriate degree of development and/or maturity for the intended product purpose. Berries are treated in an appropriate manner to obtain the above products, by undergoing operations such as threshing, sieving and sifting, soaking, washing, blanching, drying or dehydrating, decorticating, grading, crushing and grinding. 
The product shall be free from foreign odours, flavours and free from any other harmful substances and added colours.
