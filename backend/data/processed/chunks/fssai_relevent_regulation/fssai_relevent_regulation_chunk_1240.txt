2.11.3 
GELATIN shall be purified product obtained by partial hydrolysis of collagen, derived from the skin, white connective tissues and bones of animals. It shall be colourless or pale yellowish and translucent in the form of sheets, flakes, shreds or coarse to fine powder. It shall have very slight odour and taste but not objectionable which is characteristic and boluillon like. It is stable in air when dry but is subject to microbial decomposition when moist or in soluble. It shall not contain:- 
 
(a) more than 15 per cent moisture; 
 
(b) more than 3.0 per cent of total ash; 
 
(c) more than 1000 parts per million of sulphur dioxide; 
 
(d) less than 15 per cent of nitrogen, on dry weight basis.
