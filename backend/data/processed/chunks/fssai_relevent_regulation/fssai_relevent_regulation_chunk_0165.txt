62[(c) Whey Cheeses are solid, semi-solid, or soft products which are principally obtained through either of the following processes: 
(1) the concentration of whey and the moulding of the concentrated product; 
(2) the coagulation of whey by heat with or without the addition of acid. 
In each case, the whey may be pre-concentrated prior to the further concentration of whey or coagulation of the whey proteins. The process may also include the addition of milk, cream, or other raw materials of milk origin before or after concentration or coagulation. 
The ratio of whey protein to casein in the product obtained through the coagulation of whey shall be distinctly higher than that of milk. 
The product obtained through the coagulation of whey may either be ripened or unripened.
