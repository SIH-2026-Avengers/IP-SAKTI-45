2.3.18 Tamarind Pulp/Puree and Concentrate: 
 
1. 
Tamarind Pulp/Puree and Concentrate means the unfermented product which is capable of fermentation, obtained from fresh or dried tamarind, by boiling with water and sieving it, and preserved either by thermal processing or by using permitted preservatives. 
 
2. 
The Tamarind Concentrate is the product obtained from tamarind pulp/ puree from which water has been removed by evaporation to achieve appropriate concentration.
