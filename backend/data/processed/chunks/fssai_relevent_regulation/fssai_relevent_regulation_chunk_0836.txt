6. Fresh or Chilled or Frozen Rabbit meat. - 
(1) The standards specified in this clause shall apply to fresh or chilled or frozen rabbit meat which includes raw rabbit whole carcasses, pieces, cuts or edible offals that have been packed in any suitable packaging material. 
(2) Rabbit meat means the edible portion of domestic rabbits (Oryctolagus cuniculus). 
(3) Fresh rabbit meat means rabbit meat that has not been treated in any way to ensure its preservation.
