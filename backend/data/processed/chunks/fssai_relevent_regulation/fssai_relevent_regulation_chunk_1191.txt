1A.4 It shall be free from artificial colouring, flavouring, facing, extraneous matter or glazing substances and shall be in dry and fresh condition, free from rancid or obnoxious flavours. It shall conform to the following standards on dry weight basis, namely: - 
(i) 
Moisture, percent by mass, max 
5.0 
(ii) 
Total Ash, percent by mass 
3.0 to 6.0 
(iii) 
Acid insoluble ash, percent by mass, max 
0.1 
(iv) 
Water soluble ash, percent by mass, min 
65.0 
(v) 
Alkalinity of soluble ash in ml of 0.1 N hydrochloric acid per gram of material, percent by mass, ml 
3.5 to 5.0 
(vi) 
Aqueous extracts, percent by mass 
26.0 to 35.0 
(vii) 
Caffeine (anhydrous) percent by mass, max 
0.1]
