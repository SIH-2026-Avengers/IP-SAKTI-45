6.4.1 Fresh pastas and noodles and like products 
Products that are untreated (i.e. not heated, boiled, steamed, cooked, pre-gelatinized or frozen) and are no dehydrated. These products are intended to be consumed soon after preparation. Examples include unboiled noodles, and "skins" or crusts for spring rolls, wontons, and shuo mai. 
 
6.4.2 Dried pastas and noodles and like products 
Products that are untreated (i.e. not heated, boiled, steamed, cooked, pre-gelatinized or frozen) and are dehydrated. Examples include dried forms of: spaghetti, bean vermicelli, rice vermicelli, macaroni, and rice noodles.
