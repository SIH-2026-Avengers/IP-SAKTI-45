Expeller pressed edible groundnut flour shall be whitish to light brown in colour, uniform in composition and shall be free from insect or fungal infestation, objectionable odour and rancid taste. It shall not contain added flavouring and colouring agent or any other extraneous matter. It shall be free from castor husk or MAHUA oilcake. It shall be manufactured, packed, stored and distributed under hygienic conditions. It shall conform to the following standards, namely:- 
 
Moisture 
Not more than 9.0 per cent. by weight.
