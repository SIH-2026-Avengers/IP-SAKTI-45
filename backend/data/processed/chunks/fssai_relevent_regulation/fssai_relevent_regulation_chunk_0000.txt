CHAPTER 1 
 
 GENERAL 
 
1.1: Title and commencement 
 
1.1.1: These regulations may be called the Food Safety and Standards (Food Products 
Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011. 
 
1.1.2: These regulatiions shall come into force on or after 5th August, 2011, "except regulations, 
2.1.6(1)(2)(3), 2.1.7(1)(2)(3)(4), 2.1.12(1) and entries relating in table 14 of appendix A and table 2 of appendix B, which shall come into force after six months from that date and in regulation 
2.1.8(1)(3) and 2.1.11(1)(2) only relating entries in table which shall come into force after six months from that date.
