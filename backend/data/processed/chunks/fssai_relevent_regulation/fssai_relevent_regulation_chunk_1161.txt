2.9.36 Pimento or Allspice 
(1) Pimento or Allspice Whole means the dried, whole berry of Pimenta dioica (L.)Merr. It shall be dark brown in colour. It shall have a mixed flavour of four spices - clove, cinnamon, nutmeg and pepper. It shall be free from any foreign taste or odour, including rancidity or mustiness. It shall be free from living and dead insects, moulds, insect fragments and rodent contamination visible to the naked eye.
