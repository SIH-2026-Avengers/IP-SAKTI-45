4.1.2.3 Fruits in vinegar, oil, or brine 
Includes pickled products such as mango pickles, lime pickles, pickled gooseberries, plumsand pickled watermelon rind. Oriental pickled ("cured" or "preserved") fruit products are sometimes referred to as "candied" fruits. These are not the candied fruit products of category 4.1.2.7 (i.e. dried, sugar coated fruits).
