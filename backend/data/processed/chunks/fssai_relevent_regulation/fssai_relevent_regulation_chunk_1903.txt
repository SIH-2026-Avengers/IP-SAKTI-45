Explanation II (for preservatives) 
The use of more than one preservative has been allowed in the alternative, those preservatives may be used in combination with one or more alternatives, provided the quantity of each preservative so used does not exceed such number of parts out of those specified for that preservative of the aforesaid tables as may be worked out on the basis of the proportion in which such preservatives are combined.
