51[2.8.3: Honey and it's by products: 
1. Honey. - 
77[(I) Honey is the natural sweet substance produced by honey bees from the nectar of plants or from secretions of living parts of plants or excretions of plant sucking insects on the living parts of plants, which the bees collect, transform by combining with specific substances of their own, deposit, dehydrate, store and leave in the honey comb to ripen and mature. 
(a) Blossom Honey or Nectar Honey is the honey which comes from nectars of plants. 
(b) Honeydew Honey is the honey which comes mainly from excretions of plant sucking insects 
(Hemiptera) on the living parts of plants or secretions of living parts of plants]
