4. Dried or Dehydrated Meat Products. - 
 
(1) The standards specified in this clause shall apply to dried or dehydrated meat products which have been packed in any suitable packing material. This category describes several treatment methods (e.g., curing, salting, pickling and drying) that preserve and extend the shelf life of the meat and meat products.
