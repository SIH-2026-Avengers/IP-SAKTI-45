2.4.15 BAKERY PRODUCTS: 
 
73[1. Biscuit.- (1) Biscuit is a baked product leavened or non-leavened , coated or uncoated, center- filled partially or wholly such as but not limited to wafer biscuits, coated wafers, cookies, crackers, centre-filled biscuits, enrobed biscuits, sandwich biscuits, crème biscuit including fat free/ low fat or sugar free/ low sugar variants.
