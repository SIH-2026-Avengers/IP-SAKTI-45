2.9.33 TEJPAT.-(1)"Tejpat" means the dried leaves of the tree Cinnamomumtamala, Nees and 
Ebermof family lauraceae, which shall have characteristic aroma and shall be clean and free from musty odour, off-flavor, mould growth, insect infestation, rodent contamination and other impurities except to the extent as per the requirements given below. 
(2) It shall be free from admixture of leaves other than Tejpat. 
(3) It shall conform to the following requirements, namely:-
