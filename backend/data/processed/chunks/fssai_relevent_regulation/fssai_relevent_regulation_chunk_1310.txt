Spectrophotometry-Absorption maxima of aqueous solution shall be at 220 to 225, 266, 371 and 444 mu. 
Specific Rotation-It shall be determined in a 0.5 per cent w/v solution in a mixture of 1.5 ml of 
0.1 N alcoholic solution of potassium hydroxide (free from carbonate) and sufficient freshly boiled and cooled water to produce 10 ml. The specific rotation, when calculated with reference to the substance dried to constant weight in the dark at 105oC, shall be,- 122oC. 
 
The material shall have minimum purity of 97.0 per cent.
