For fish, heads and gills shall be completely removed, scales and tail may be removed. The fish may be eviscerated. If eviscerated, it shall be practically free from visceral parts other roe, milt or kidney. If ungutted, it shall be practically free from undigested feed or used feed. For shrimps, heads, shell, antennae shall be completely removed. 
(ii) Other Ingredients- 
The packing medium and all other ingredients used shall be of food grade quality and conform to all applicable standards prescribed in these regulations. No artificial colouring matter and firming agents shall be used. 
(iii) Decomposition- 
The total volatile base nitrogen (TVBN) level of raw material (fin fish or shell fish) should not exceed 35mg/100g.
