5. Cooked or Semi-Cooked Meat Products. - 
 
(1) The standards specified in this clause shall apply to cooked or Semi-cooked meat products which includes cooked (including cured and cooked) and heat treated meat products. 
 (2) Cooked meat means the meat or meat product subjected to heat treatment, wherein minimum thermal core temperature of 75 C is achieved. 
(3) Semi cooked meat means partially heat treated meat or meat product that will require additional cooking before consumption.
