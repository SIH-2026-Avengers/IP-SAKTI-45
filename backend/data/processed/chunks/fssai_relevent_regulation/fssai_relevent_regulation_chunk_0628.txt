2.4.5 
Pearl Barley (Jau) 
 
1. 
Pearl Barley (Jau) shall be the product obtained from sound and clean barley (Horbeum vulgare or hordeum distichon). It shall be whitish in colour and shall be free from fermented, musty or other objectionable taste or odour, adulterants and insect and fungus infestation and rodent contamination. It shall not contain other foodgrains more than 1 per cent by weight. 
 
Barley powder shall be the product obtained by grinding clean and sound dehusked barley 
(Hordeum vulgare or Hordeum distichon) grains. Barley starches shall not be less than 98.0 per cent by weight.
