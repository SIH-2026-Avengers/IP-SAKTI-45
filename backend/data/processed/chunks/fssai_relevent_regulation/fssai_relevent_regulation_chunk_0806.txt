(2) Fermented Soybean Curd (made with S. thermophillus + L. bulgaricus) is prepared by lactic acid fermentation of aqueous extract of soybean using mixed cultures of 2 strains, namely, 
Streptococcus thermophillus and Lactobacillus bulgaricus. Fermented Soybean Curd (made with 
S. thermophillus + L. bulgaricus) may be plain or sweetened and/or flavored. It may contain fruits mango/ pine apple/ orange or other fruits. Milk/reconstituted milk may be added in the aqueous extract of soybean. If added, it shall not exceed 25% of the final product.
