(3) The product is prepared from fish eggs of sturgeon fishes belonging to the Acipenseridae family 
(four genera Acipenser, Huso, Pseudoscaphirhynchus and Scaphirhynchus and hybrid species of these genera). 
 
(4) The eggs are of about one size and characteristically coloured according to the species used. 
Colour can vary from light grey to black or from light yellow to yellowish grey. Brownish and greenish shades are permissible. 
 
(5) The product is made with addition of salt and is intended for direct human consumption. The salt content of the product shall be in the range of 3-5 g/100gm in the end product.
