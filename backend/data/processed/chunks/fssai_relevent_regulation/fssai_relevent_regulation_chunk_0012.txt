(g) 
"Pasteurization, Pasteurized and similar terms" means a microbicidal heat treatment aimed at reducing the number of any pathogenic micro-organisms in milk and liquid milk products, if present, to a level at which they do not constitute a significant health hazard. Pasteurization conditions shall be designed to effectively destroy the organisms Mycobacterium tuberculosis and Coxiella burnettii.
