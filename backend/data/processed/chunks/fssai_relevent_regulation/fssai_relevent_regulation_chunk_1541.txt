8.2.1.3 Fermented non-heat treated processed meat and poultry products in whole pieces or cuts 
Fermented products are a type of pickled product produced by the action of lactic acid bacteria in the presence of salt. 
 
8.2.2 Heat-treated processed meat and poultry products in whole pieces or cuts 
Includes cooked (including cured and cooked, and dried and cooked), heat-treated (including sterilized) and canned meat cuts. 
 
77[8.2.3 Frozen raw, flavored/marinated, processed meat and poultry products in whole pieces or cuts - 
Includes raw, flavoured/marinated raw and cooked meat cuts that have been frozen.] 
 
8.3 Processed comminuted meat andpoultry products 
Includes various treatments for non-heat treated products (8.3.1) and heat-treated products (8.3.2).
