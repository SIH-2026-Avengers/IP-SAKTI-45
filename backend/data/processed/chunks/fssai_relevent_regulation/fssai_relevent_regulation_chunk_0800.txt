77[2.4.37 Multigrain flour (atta) 
1. Multigrain flour (Atta): Multigrain flour (atta) means the product obtained by milling/grinding blend of clean whole wheat and other permitted ingredients or mixing their flours. The product shall have flour composition obtained from whole wheat ranging from 
50-90 % and rest of the flour (10% - 50%) composition shall be from other permitted ingredients singly or in combination. The product shall be free from abnormal flavours, odours, living insects, visible mould, filth (impurities of animal origins, including dead insects).
