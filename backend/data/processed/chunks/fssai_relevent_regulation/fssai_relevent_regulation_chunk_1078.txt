2.9.8: Cumin (Zeera, Kalonji) 
 
73[1. Cumin (Safed Zeera) whole.- means the dried mature seeds of Cuminumcyminum L. of the 
Apiaceae family. It shall have characteristic colour, aroma and flavour. It shall be free from live insects, any foreign odour or flavour and mustiness. It shall be free from added colour and harmful substances.
