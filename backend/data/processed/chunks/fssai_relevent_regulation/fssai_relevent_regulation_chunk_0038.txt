2.1.3 Standard for Flavoured Milk 
This Standard applies to Flavoured Milk as defined in item 1 of this sub-regulation. 
1. 
Description. - 
"Flavoured Milk" means the product prepared from milk or other products derived from milk, or both, and edible flavourings with or without addition of sugar, nutritive sweeteners, other non-dairy ingredients including, stabilisers and food colours. Flavoured milk shall be subjected to heat treatment as provided in sub-regulation 2.1.1 (General Standards for Milk and Milk Products). 
 
Where flavoured milk is dried or concentrated, the dried or concentrated product on addition of prescribed amount of water shall give a product conforming to the requirements of flavoured milk.
