Synonyms -4, 1 '6'-Trichlorogalactosucrose; INS 955 
 
Chemical formula - 
C12H19CI3O8 Molecular weight- 397.64 
It shall be white to off-white, odourless, crystalline powder, having a sweet taste. It shall be freely soluble in water, in methanol and in alcohol and slightly soluble in ethyl acetate. It shall contain not less than 98.0% and not more than 102.0% of C12H19CI3O8 calculated on anhydrous basis. It shall not contain more than 3PPM of Arsenic (as AS) and 10PPM or heavy metals (as Pb). It shall not contain more than 0.1% of methanol. Residue on ignition shall not be more than 0.7% and 73[water not more than 2.0%]
