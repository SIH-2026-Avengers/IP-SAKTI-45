(2). Soybean Curd and Related Products: 
(a) Semisolid soybean curd: Semi solid soybean curd is the semisolid product in which soybean protein is coagulated by adding coagulant into the soybean liquid. It may be coagulated using magnesium chloride (nigari), calcium sulfate, calcium chloride, citric acid, acetic acid, magnesium sulfate and glucono -δ- lactone.
