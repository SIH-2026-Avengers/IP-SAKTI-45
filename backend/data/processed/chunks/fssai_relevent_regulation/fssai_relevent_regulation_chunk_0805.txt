79[2.4.39 FERMENTED SOYBEAN CURD AND FERMENTED SOYBEAN CURD (made with 
S. thermophillus + L. bulgaricus).- (1) Fermented Soybean Curd is prepared by fermentation of aqueous extract of soybean. The fermentation is carried out by the mixed cultures of Lactic acid bacteria or any other suitable cultures. It may be plain or sweetened and/or flavored. Milk/ reconstituted milk may be added in the aqueous extract of soybean. If added, it shall not exceed 
25% of the final product.
