12.1 Salt and salt substitutes 
Includes salt (12.1.1) and salt substitutes (12.1.2) used as seasoning for food. 
 
12.1.1 Salt 
Primarily food-grade sodium chloride. Includes table salt, iodized and fluoride iodized salt, and dendritic salt. This category also includes similar traditional products like black salt, rock salt 
(sendhanamak, kala namak, Gumma namak) sea saltetc.
