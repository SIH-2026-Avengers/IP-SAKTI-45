(a) 
(i) "Vanilla pods" means whole pods which may be split. 
 
(ii) The pods shall be sound, dry or wooded, of typical flavour, reddish in colour and which may have few to several stains, the total length of which doesnot exceed half the length of the pod. 
(iii) It shall not be insect infested, mouldy, creosoted, blistered or oxidized and free from bird and rodent damage.
