(c) Definitions: 
(i) 
"Damaged fruit" means fruit affected by any damage or blemish on the surface resulting from factors such as hail, etc., affecting more than 5 mm
2 of fruit surface. 
(ii) 
"Broken fruit" means fruit affected by any damage resulting from improper halving or other mechanical action. 
(iii) 
"Immature fruit" means fruit which is generally deficient in sugar and may be sour in taste. 
(iv) 
"Insect damaged fruit" means fruit which is affected by insect damage or containing dead insects, mites or other pests. 
(v) 
"Mouldy fruit" means fruit which is affected by mould to a visible extent, or decay. 
(vi) 
"Dirty fruit" means fruit affected by imbedded dirt or any other foreign material.
