25. Gelatin from Fish Processing Waste.- (1)Gelatin is derived from collagen, which is a natural structural protein, predominantly found in connective tissue of fish and terrestrial animals. Collagen is the most ubiquitous of animal proteins. The fish processing waste comprising of skin, bones, swim bladder and scales are rich in collagen content.
