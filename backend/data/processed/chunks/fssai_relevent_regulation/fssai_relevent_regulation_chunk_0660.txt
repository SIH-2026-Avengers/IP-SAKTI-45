23. Whole and decorticated pearl millet grains (Bajra): (1) Pearl millet grains (whole or decorticated grains) shall be the dried mature grains of Pennisetum typhoideum Rich or Pennisetum americanum L. 
(2) Whole grains.- The whole grains of pearl millet obtained as such after proper threshing with no mechanical treatment.
