27[2.4.16 EXPELLER PRESSED FLOUR 
 
2. Expeller Pressed Edible Groundnut Flour means the product obtained by expeller pressing fresh, clean degermed groundnut kernels which have been decuticled after mild roasting. The kernels shall be sorted and selected either by visual inspection, inspection under ultraviolet light, electronic sorting or by other means. The kernels shall be free from insect or fungal infestation.
