(b) 
 When sugars are added to water or water and one or more fruit juices the liquid media shall be classified on the basis of the cut-out strength as follows: 
1. Slightly Sweetened Water/ Extra 
Light Syrup 
Not less than 10° Brix and not more than 13.9° Brix; 
2. Light Syrup 
Not less than 14° Brix and not more than 17.9° Brix 
3. Heavy Syrup 
Not less than 18° Brix and not more than 21.9° Brix; 
4. Extra Heavy Syrup 
Not less than 22° Brix.
