1. Description 
Edible Fungi Products means products prepared from fresh edible fungi. The products may be dried edible fungi (including freeze-dried fungi, fungi grits, fungi powder), pickled fungi, salted fungi, fermented fungi, fungi in vegetable oils, quick frozen fungi, sterilized fungi, fungi extract, fungi concentrate and dried fungi concentrate. 
Edible fungi products shall be clean, undamaged, free, as far as possible, of maggot damage and possess the flavour and taste appropriate to the species. 
Edible Fungi products may contain salt (sodium chloride), vinegar, spices and herbs, sugars (any carbohydrate sweetening matter), refined edible vegetable oil, refined edible animal fat, butter, milk, milk powder, cream, water or any other suitable ingredients whose standards are prescribed in Food 
Safety & Standards (Food Product Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011.
