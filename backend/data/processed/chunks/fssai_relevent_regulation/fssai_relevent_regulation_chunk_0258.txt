2. 
Cotton seed oil (binola ka tel) means the oil extracted from clean, sound delinted and decorticated cotton seeds (genus Gossypium). It shall be refined. It shall be clear, free from rancidity, suspended or other foreign matter, separated water, added colouring or flavouring substances or mineral oil. It shall conform to the following standards:- 
 
Butyro-refractometer reading at 40oC 
55.6 to 60.2 
 
OR 
Refractive Index at 40oC 
1.4630-1.4660
