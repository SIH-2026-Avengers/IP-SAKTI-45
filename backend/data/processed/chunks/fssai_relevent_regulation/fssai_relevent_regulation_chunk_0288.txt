13. 
NIGER SEED OIL (Sargiya ka tel) means the edible oil obtained by process of expressing clean and sound seeds of Guizotia abyssinica. It shall be clear and free from rancidity, suspended or other foreign matter, separated water, added colouring or flavouring substances, mineral or other oil. 
It shall conform to the following standards:- 
 
Butyro-refractometer reading at 40 °C 
61.0-65.0 
Or 
Refractive Index at 40oC 
1.4665-1.4691 
Saponification value 
188-193 
Iodine value 
110 to 135
