3. 
Pistachio Nuts means the product obtained from mature seeds of Pistacia vera L which have been sun dried and their shells opened naturally or mechanically. The product may be raw, roasted, salted and/or lime juice treated. The product shall be free from foreign matter, living insects, mould, dead insects, insect fragments and rodent contamination.
