2.4.28. Sago flour is the product prepared from the pith or soft core of sago palm tree 
(Metroxylon sp.) or the Sago of Tapioca (Manihot utilissima). The product shall be free from off- flavours and odours. It must be free from filth (impurities of animal origin including insects) and other extraneous matters. Colour shall be white to light-brown.
