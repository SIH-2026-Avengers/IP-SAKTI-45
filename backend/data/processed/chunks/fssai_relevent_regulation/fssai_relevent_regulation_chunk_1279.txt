(9) Specifications for the Identity and Purity of Food Additives 
Food additives used in accordance with these regulations shall be of appropriate food grade quality and should at all times conform with the applicable Specifications of Identity and Purity recommended under these regulations and in terms of safety, food grade quality is achieved by conformance of additives to their specifications as a whole (not merely with individual criteria) and through their production, storage, transport, and handling in accordance with Good 
Manufacturing Practice (GMP).
