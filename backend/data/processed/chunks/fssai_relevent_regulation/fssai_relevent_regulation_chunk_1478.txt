1.6.4.1 Plain processed cheese 
Processed cheese product that does not contain added flavours, seasonings, fruit, vegetables and/or meat. Examples include American cheese, Requeson etc. 
 
1.6.4.2 Flavoured processed cheese, including containing fruit, vegetables, meat, etc. 
Processed cheese product that contains added flavours, seasonings, fruit, vegetables and/or meat such asNeufchatel cheese spread with vegetables, pepper jack cheese, cheddar cheese spread with wine, and cheese balls (formed processed cheese coated in nuts, herbs or spices). 
 
1.6.5 Cheese analogues 
Products that look like cheese, but in which milk fat has been partly or completely replaced by other fats which includes imitation cheese, imitation cheese mixes, and imitation cheese powders.
