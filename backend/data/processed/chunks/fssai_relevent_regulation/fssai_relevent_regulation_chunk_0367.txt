2.2.6 HYDROGENATED VEGETABLE OILS 
 
1. Vanaspati means any refined edible vegetable oil or oils, subjected to a process of hydrogenation in any form 2[or chemical or enzymatic interesterification]. It shall be prepared by hydrogenation from groundnut oil, cottonseed oil and sesame oil or mixtures thereof or any other harmless vegetable oils allowed by the government for the purpose. Refined sal seed fat, if used, shall not be more than 10 per cent of the total oil mix.
