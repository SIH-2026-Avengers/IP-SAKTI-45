72[2.3.64 WATER CHESTNUT FLOUR (SINGHARE KA ATTA). - 
(1) Water Chestnut flour means the product obtained by grinding clean, sound and dried nuts of 
Trapabispinosa or Trapaquadrispinosaspecies commonly known as Singhara. It shall be white in colour, and shall be free from rancid and objectionable odour, extraneous matter, insects, fungus, rodent hair and excreta. It shall be free from added colour and flavour. It shall conform to the following standards, namely: -
