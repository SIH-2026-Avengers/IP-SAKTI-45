4. Liquid Egg Products 
(1) The standard specified in this clause shall apply to egg products designated as "Liquid Egg 
White", "Liquid Egg Yolk" and "Liquid Whole Egg" prepared from hens' (Gallus gallus) eggs packaged in any suitable packaging material. 
(2) For the purpose of this clause,- 
(a)Liquid egg product means the whole egg, egg yolk /or egg white, which is pasteurized and preserved using approved preservatives e.g beta-Hydroxy Butyric acid, Lactic acid or 
Succinic acid. 
(b)Liquid whole egg means the homogeneous product obtained from the complete contents of broken out hens eggs. 
(c) Liquid Egg yolk means the homogeneous product produced from the separation of the yolk of broken out hens-eggs. 
(d) Liquid Egg albumen means the homogeneous product obtained from the separation of the white of broken out hens egg.
