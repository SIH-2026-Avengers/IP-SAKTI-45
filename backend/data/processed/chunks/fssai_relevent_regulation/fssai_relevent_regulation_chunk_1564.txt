11.6 Table-top sweeteners, including those containing high-intensity sweeteners 
Includes products that are preparations of high-intensity sweeteners (e.g. acesulfame potassium, steviols) and/or ofpolyols (e.g. sorbitol). These products, which are sold to the final consumer, may be in powder, solid (e.g. tablets or cubes), or liquid form.
