72[24. 77[Multi-Source Edible Oil] means an admixture of any two edible vegetable oils where the proportion by weight of any edible vegetable oil used in the admixture is not less than 20 per cent. 
The individual oils in the blend shall conform to the respective standards prescribed by these regulations. 
77[Multi-Source Edible Oil] shall not contain more than 33% of saturated fatty acids. 
77[Multi-Source Edible Oil] may have an ideal ratio of omega 3 and omega 6 to be in the range of 1:5 to 1:10. Third oil namely Chia oil and/or Flaxseed/Linseed Oil, upto 5 % of the total oil, may be added if the 77[Multi-Source Edible Oil] is claimed to have an ideal ratio of omega 3: omega 6.
