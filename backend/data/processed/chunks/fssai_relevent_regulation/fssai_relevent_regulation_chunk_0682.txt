2.4.8. CORN FLAKES: 
 
51[1. Corn flakes means the product obtained from dehulled, degermed and cook corn (Zea mays L.) by flaking, partially drying and toasting. It may contain any other permitted ingredients appropriate to the product whose standards are prescribed in these regulations. It shall be in the form of crisp flakes of reasonably uniform size and golden brown in colour. It shall be free from dirt, insects, larvae and impurities and any other extraneous matter.
