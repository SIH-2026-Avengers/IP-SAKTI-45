7.1.4 Bread-type products, including bread stuffing and bread crumbs 
Includes bread-based products such as croutons, bread stuffing and stuffing mixes, and prepared doughs (e.g. for biscuits, toasted bread (rusks), prepared doughs for bread/bread-type products including their frozen counterparts etc.). Bread mixes are included in category 7.1.6.
