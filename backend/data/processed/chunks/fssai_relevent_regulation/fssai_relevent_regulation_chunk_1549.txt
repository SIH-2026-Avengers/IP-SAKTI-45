9.2.4.3 Fried fish and fish products, including molluscs, crustaceans, and echinoderms 
Ready-to-eat products prepared from fish or fish portions, with or without further dressing in eggs and breadcrumbs or batter, that are fried, baked, roasted or barbecued, and then packaged or canned with or without sauce or oil. Examples include ready-to-eat fried surimi, fried calamari, and fried soft-shell crabs.
