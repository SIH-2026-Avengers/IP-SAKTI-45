Glycerol esters of wood rosins commonly known as ester-gum is hard yellow to pale amber coloured solid. It is a complex mixture of tri and diglycerol esters of rosin acids from wood rosin. 
It is produced by the esterification of pale wood rosin with food grade glycerol. It is composed of approximately 90 per cent rosin acids and 10 per cent neutrals (non-acidic compounds). The resin acid fraction is a complex mixture of isomeric diterpeniod monocarboxylic acids having the typical molecular formula of C20 H30 O2 chiefly abietic acid. The substance is purified by steam stripping or by counter-current steam distillation. 
 
Identification: 
Solubility-Insoluble in water, soluble in acetone and in Benzene. 
 
Infra Red Spectrum-Obtain the infra-red spectram of a thin film of the sample deposited on a potassium bromide plate-scan between 600 and 4000 wave numbers. Compare with typical spectrum obtained from pure ester-gum.
