9.0 Fish and fish products, including molluscs, crustaceans, and echinoderms 
This broad category is divided into categories for fresh fish (9.1) and various processed fish products 
(9.2- 9.4). This category includes aquatic vertebrates (e.g. fish) andaquatic invertebrates (e.g. jellyfish), as well as molluscs (e.g. clams, snails), crustaceans (e.g. shrimp, crab, lobster), and echinoderms (e.g. sea urchins, sea cucumbers). Fish products may be treated with coatings, such as glazes and spice rubs, prior to marketing to the consumer (e.g. glazed frozen fish fillets).
