Preservatives shall not be added to sample units intended for microbiological examination. The desired number of sample units as per sampling plan given in Table-9A & 9B shall be taken from same batch/lot and shall be submitted to the notified laboratory. Three sets, each containing 'n' number of samples (n as defined in the sampling plan eg if n=5, then total no. of samples is 15) shall be drawn. Each of these three sets shall be tested in three different accredited laboratories. The final decision shall be based on the results of three accredited laboratories.
