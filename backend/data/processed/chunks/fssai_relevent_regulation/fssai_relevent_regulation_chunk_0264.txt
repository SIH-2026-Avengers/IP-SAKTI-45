5. 
Mahua oil means the oil expressed from clean and sound seeds or nuts of Madhuca 
(Bassi latifolia or B. longifolia or a mixture of both). It shall be clear and shall be free from rancidity, suspended or other foreign matter, separated water, added colouring or flavouring substances, or mineral oil. It shall be refined and shall conform to the following standards:- 
 
Butyro-refractometer reading at 40oC 
49.5 to 52.7 
 
Or 
Refractive Index at 40oC 
1.4590 - 1.4611 
Saponification value 
187 to 196
