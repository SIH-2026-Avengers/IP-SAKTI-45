GMP, except that 
Phosphorus not to exceed 
4 per cent. m/m and NH4+ 
3 per cent. m/m of the salt substitute mixture

Magnesium salts of adipic, glutamic, carbonic, citric, succinic, acetic, tartaric, lactic, hydro- chloric or orthophosphoric acids, mixed with other Mg-free salt substitutes as listed in 
6.(1)(a), 6.(1)(c) and 6.(1)(d), and/or 
 
Mg++to be not more than 20 per cent. m/m of the total of the cations K+, 
Ca++and NH4+ present in the salt substitute mixture and Phosphorus not to exceed 4 per cent. m/m of the salt substitute mixture

Choline salts of acetic, carbonic, lactic, tartaric, citric or hydrochloric acids, mixed with other choline-free salt
