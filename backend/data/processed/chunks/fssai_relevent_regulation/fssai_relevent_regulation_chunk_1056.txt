2.9.2: Cardamom (Elaichi) 
 
1. 
Cardamom (Chhoti Elaichi) Whole means the dried capsules of nearly ripe fruits of 
Elettaria cardamomum L. Maton Var. Minuscula Burkill. The capsules may be light green to brown or pale cream to white when bleached with sulphur dioxide. It shall have characteristic flavour free from any foreign odour, mustiness or rancidity. It shall be free from mould, living and dead insects, insect fragments, rodent contamination. Thrip marks alone should not lead to the conclusion that the capsules have been infested with insects. The product shall be free from added colouring matter and other harmful substances. 
 
It shall conform to the following standards: 
 
(i) 
Extraneous matter 
Not more than 1.0 percent by weight 
(ii) Empty and malformed capsules by count 
Not more than 3.0 percent by count 
(iii) Immature and shrivelled capsules 
Not more than 3.0 percent by weight 
(iv) Moisture 
Not more than 13.0 percent by weight
