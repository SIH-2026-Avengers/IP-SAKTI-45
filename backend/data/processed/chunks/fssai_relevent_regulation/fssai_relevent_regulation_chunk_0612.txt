4. Definition of Defects a. Damaged fungi means fungi with more than quarter of the cap missing. b. Carbonized fungi means whole or cut dried fungi with traces of carbonization on their surface c. Crushed fungi means parts of fungi passing through a sieve having a 15 x 15 mm mesh for fresh fungi and a 5 x 5 mm mesh for dried fungi. d. Spoiled fungi means fungi which are brownish or rotten as a result of attack by microorganisms and/or mould. e. Maggot damaged fungi means fungi having holes caused by maggots. f. Seriously maggot damaged fungi means fungi having four or more holes caused by maggots.
