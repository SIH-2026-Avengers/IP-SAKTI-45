Test for absence of tall oil rosin (Sulphur test)-Pass the test as given below: 
 
When sulphur-containing organic compounds are heated in the presence of sodium formate, the sulphur is converted to hydrogen sulfide which can readily be detected by the use of lead acetate paper. A positive test indicates the use of tall oil rosin instead of wood rosin. 
 
Apparatus-Test Tube: Use a standard, 10x75 mm, heat-resistant, glass test tube, Burner - 
Bunsen: A small size burner of the microflame type is preferred. 
 
Reagents 
 
Sodium Formate Solution: Dissolve 20g of reagent grade sodium formate, NaOOCH, in 
100 ml of distilled water. Lead Acetate Test Paper: Commercially available from most chemical supply houses.
