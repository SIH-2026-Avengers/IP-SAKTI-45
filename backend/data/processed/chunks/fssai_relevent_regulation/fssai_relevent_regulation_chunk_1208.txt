(I) 
Water used in preparation of caffeinated beverages should conform to the standards of packaged drinking water as prescribed in regulation 2.10.8 72[or mineral water as prescribed in regulations 2.10.7] of the Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food 
Additives) Regulations, 2011. 
(II) 
Essential Composition: It shall contain not less than 145mg per liter and not more than 300 mg per litre total caffeine from whatever sources it may be derived in the formulation of the product.
