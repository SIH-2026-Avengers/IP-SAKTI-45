1. Description. - 
 
Milk fat, ghee, butter oil, anhydrous milk fat and anhydrous butter oil are fatty products derived exclusively from milk or products obtained from milk, or both, by means of processes which result in almost total removal of water and milk solids-not-fat. 
 
Ghee has especially developed flavour and physical structure as a result of its method of manufacturing. 
 
2. Essential Composition and Quality Factors.- 
 (a) Raw Material.- 
Milk and products obtained from milk. The raw material used shall be free from added flavour, colour or preservative.
