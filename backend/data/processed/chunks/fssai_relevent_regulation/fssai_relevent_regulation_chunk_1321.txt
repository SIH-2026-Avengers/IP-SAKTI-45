It shall be free from mercury, copper and chromium in any form; aromatic amines, aromatic nitro compounds, aromatic hydrocarbons, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon, 2-naphthyl aminobenzidine, amino-4-diphenyl (xenylamine) or their derivatives and cyanides. 
 
The total coal tar dye content percent by mass (m/v) in colour preparation or in mixture shall be declared on the lable of the container. In powder preparation, the declared value shall be on moisture free basis and in case of liquid preparation on ' as is basis' and the total dye content shall within ± 15 percent of the declared value.
