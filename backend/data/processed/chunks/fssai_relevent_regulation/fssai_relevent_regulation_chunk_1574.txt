12.7 Salads and sandwich spreads excluding cocoa- and nut-based spreads of food categories 
4.2.2.5 and 5.1.3 
Includes prepared salads (e.g. macaroni salad, potato salad), milk-based sandwich spreads, non- standardized mayonnaise-like sandwich spreads, and dressings etc. 
12.8 Yeast and like products: 
Includes baker's yeast and leaven used in the manufacture of baked goods. Includes the products used in the production of alcoholic beverages. 
 
12.9 Soybean-based seasonings and condiments 
Includes products that are derived from soybeans and other ingredients intended for use as seasonings andcondiments, such as fermented soybean paste and soybean sauces.
