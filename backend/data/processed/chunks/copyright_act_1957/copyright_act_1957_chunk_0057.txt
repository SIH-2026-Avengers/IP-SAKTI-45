26. Term of copyright in cinematograph films.- In the case of a cinematograph film, copyright shall subsist until 2[sixty years] from the beginning of the calendar year next following the year in which the film is published. 
27. Term of copyright in sound recording.- In the case of a 3[sound recording] copyright shall subsist until 2[sixty years] from the beginning of the calendar year next following the year in which the 3[sound recording] is published. 
28. Term of copyright in Government works.- In the case of a Government work, where Government is the first owner of the copyright therein, copyright shall subsist until 2[sixty years] from the beginning of the calendar year next following the year in which the work is first published.
