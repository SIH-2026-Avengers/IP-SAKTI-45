CHAPTER V 
TERM OF COPYRIGHT 
22. Term of copyright in published literary, dramatic, musical and artistic works.- Except as otherwise hereinafter provided, copyright shall subsist in any literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work 3[***] published within the lifetime of the author until 4[sixty years] from the beginning of the calendar year next following the year in which the author dies.
