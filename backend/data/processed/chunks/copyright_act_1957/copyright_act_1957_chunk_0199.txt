74. Registrar of Copyrights and 1[Appellate Board] to possess certain powers of civil courts.- The 
Registrar of Copyrights and the 1[Appellate Board] shall have the powers of a civil court when trying a suit under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (5 of 1908), in respect of the following matters, namely, - 
(a) summoning and enforcing the attendance of any person and examining him on oath;
