2[52A. Particulars to be included in 3[sound recording] and video films.- (1) No person shall publish a 3[sound recording] in respect of any work unless the following particulars are displayed on the 
3[sound recording] and on any container thereof, namely:- 
(a) the name and address of the person who has made the sound recording; 
(b) the name and address of the owner of the copyright in such work; and 
(c) the year of its first publication.
