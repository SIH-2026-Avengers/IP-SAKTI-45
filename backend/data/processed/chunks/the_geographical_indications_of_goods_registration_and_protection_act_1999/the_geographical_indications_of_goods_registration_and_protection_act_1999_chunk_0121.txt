82. Declarations as to title of geographical indication not registerable under the Registration 
Act, 1908.-Notwithstanding anything contained in the Registration Act, 1908 (16 of 1908), no document declaring or purporting to declare any title of a person to a geographical indication other than a registered geographical indication shall be registered under that Act.
