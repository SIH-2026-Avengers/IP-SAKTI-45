CHAPTER VII 
APPEALS TO THE APPELLATE BOARD 
31. Appeals to the Appellate Board.-(1) Any person aggrieved by an order or decision of the 
Registrar under this Act, or the rules made thereunder, may prefer an appeal to the Appellate Board within three months from the date on which the order or decision sought to be appealed against is communicated to such person preferring the appeal.
