61. Exercise of discretionary power by Registrar.-Subject to the provisions of section 64, the 
Registrar shall not exercise any discretionary or other power vested in him by this Act or the rules made thereunder adversely to a person applying for the exercise of that power without (if so required by that person within the prescribed time) giving to the person an opportunity of being heard. 
62. Evidence before Registrar.-In any proceeding under this Act before the Registrar, evidence shall be given by affidavit: 
Provided that the Registrar may, if he thinks fit, take oral evidence in lieu of, or in addition to, such evidence by affidavit.
