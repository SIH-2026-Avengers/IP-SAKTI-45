CHAPTER VII PROVISIONS FOR SECRECY OF CERTAIN INVENTIONS 
 
35. Secrecy directions relating to inventions relevant for defence purposes. 
(1) Where, in respect of an application made before or after the commencement of this Act for a patent, it appears to the Controller that the invention is one of a class notified to him by the Central 
Government as relevant for defence purposes, or, where otherwise the invention appears to him to be so relevant, he may give directions for prohibiting or restricting the publication of information with respect to the invention or the communication of such information . 
 
(2) Where the Controller gives any such directions as are referred to in sub-section (1), he shall give notice of the application and of the directions to the Central Government, and the Central
