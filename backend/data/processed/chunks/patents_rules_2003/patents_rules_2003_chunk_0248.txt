CHAPTER XIX APPEALS 
 
117A. Appeals to High Court. 
(1) Save as otherwise expressly provided in sub-section (2), no appeal shall lie from any decision, order or direction made or issued under this Act by the Central Government, or from any act or order of the Controller for the purpose of giving effect to any such decision, order or direction. 
 
(2) An appeal shall lie to the High Court from any decision, order or direction of the Controller of Central Government under section 
15, section 16, section 17, section 18, section 19, section 20, sub- section (4) of section 25, section 28, section 51, section 54, section 57, section 60, section 61, section 63, section 66, sub- section (3) of section 69, section 78, sub-sections (1) to (5) of section 84, section 85, section 88, section 91, section 92 and section 94.
