143. Restrictions upon publication of specification. 
Subject to the provisions of Chapter VII, an application for a patent, and any specification file in pursuance thereof, shall not, except with the consent of the applicant, be published by the 
Controller before the expiration of the period prescribed under sub- section (1) of section 11A or before the same is open to public inspection in pursuance of sub-section (3) of section 11A or section 
43.
