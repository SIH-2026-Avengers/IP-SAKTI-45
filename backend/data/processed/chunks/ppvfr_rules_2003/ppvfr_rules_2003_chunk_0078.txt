64. Alteration of denomination of a registered variety under section 38.(1) An application, to delete any part or to add or to alter the denomination of a registered variety, under sub-section (1) of section 38, shall be made by the breeder to the Registrar in Form PV-23 of the First Schedule. 
(2) The Registrar may determine whether and subject to what conditions, if any, the amendments shall be allowed.
