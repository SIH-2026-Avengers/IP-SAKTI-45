48. Application and procedure for varying or cancelling terms of registration under section 28.-(1) 
An application under clause (a), (b), (c), (d) or (e) of sub-section (9) of section 28 for variation or cancellation of the terms of registration of a registered breeder or his successor or any other person shall be in Form PV-10 of the First Schedle.
