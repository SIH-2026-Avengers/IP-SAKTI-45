CHAPTER IX MISCELLANEOUS 
75. Manner of authorizing registered agent or registered licensee under section 81.-(1) A breeder of a variety or its propagating material or essentially derived variety or its propagating material registered under the Act, may make an application under section 81, in Form PV-32 of the First Schedule, for authorizing the registered agent or registered licensee or his assignee to institute appropriate proceedings in any court of law on his behalf.
