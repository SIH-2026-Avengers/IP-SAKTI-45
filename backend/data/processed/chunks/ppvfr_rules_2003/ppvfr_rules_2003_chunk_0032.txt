23. Matters to be included in the National Register of Plant Varieties.-The National Register of Plant 
Varieties shall contain the following particulars of each registered variety, namely:- 
(1) Registration Number; 
(2) Nationality of Breeder(s); (3) Denomination as granted; 
(4) Date of Grant of Registration; 
(5) Date on which application was received; 
(6) Provisional number given to the application; (7) Date of Gazette notification; 
(8) Grouping of the plant variety (new, extant or farmers);
