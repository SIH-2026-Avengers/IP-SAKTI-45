(8) (a) The security referred to in sub-section (8) of section 21 shall be payable as an amount decided by the

(b) In case the opposition is found to be frivolous, the Registrar may direct payment of cost as determined by him to the applicant from out of the security amount received and the balance of the security amount shall be deposited in the Authority. 
(c) In case the opposition succeeds, the security amount shall be refunded to the opposition party. 
32. Compliance with time schedule.-(1) The time schedule provided for advertisement, opposition, defence, hearing and amendment of specification under these rules shall not be extended and failure in compliance with these time schedules shall forfeit the opportunity granted.
