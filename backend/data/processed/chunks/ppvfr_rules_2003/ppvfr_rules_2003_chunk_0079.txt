65. Procedure on application for alteration of denomination under rule 64.-(1) Any interested person may, within three months from the date of advertisement of an application for alteration in denomination of a registered variety, under sub-section (2) of section 38, give a notice of opposition to the proposed change in denomination of a registered variety in Form PV -24 of the First Schedule.
