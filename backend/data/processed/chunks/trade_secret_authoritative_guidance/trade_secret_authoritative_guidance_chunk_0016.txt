Enforcement of Trade Secrets according TRIPS Agreement
Art. 1.2 TRIPS: TS are included in the categories of IP
Art. 41 TRIPS: Enforcement of IP applies to misappropriation and misuse of trade secrets 
A trade secret owner shall take action against alleged misapropriation
Regional/national laws provide different enforcement procedures
Attention: even if there is no term of protection for a TS enforcement of a misapropriation may be limited in time
