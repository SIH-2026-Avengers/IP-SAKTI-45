Examples of steps that may be reasonable 
TS management program may include:
Identification 
Education of employees
Identification and review periodically which employees "need to know" the TS and restrict access to only those
Application of physical and technological restrictions to access TS
Limitation and monitoring public access to buildings that house TS
Marking documents containing TS as "secret" or "confidential" 
Signing confidentiality / non-disclosure / non-compete agreements with relevant employees / outsiders who may get access to TS
