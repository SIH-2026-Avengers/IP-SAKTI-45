ARTIFICIAL OR NATURAL SHEET MATERIAL 
Note: 
(a) Includes sheets whose only characteristic features are their surface ornamentation or their texture; in particular, covering sheets such as wallpaper, linoleum, self-adhesive plastic sheets, wrapping sheets and rolls of paper, subject to the exceptions indicated under (b). 
(b) Not including writing paper, even in rolls (Cl. 19-01), or sheets used as building components such as wall panels and wainscoting (Cl. 25-01).
