24. Manner of giving notice under Section 61 
 
(1) The manner of giving notice, under clause (b) of section 61, shall be as follows namely: 
 
(i) 
The notice shall be in writing in Form VII. 
(ii) 
The person giving the notice may send it to: 
(a) If the alleged offense has taken place in a Union territory, to the 
Chairperson of the National Biodiversity Authority; and 
(b) If the alleged offence has been taken place in a State, to the 
Chairperson of the State Biodiversity Board.
