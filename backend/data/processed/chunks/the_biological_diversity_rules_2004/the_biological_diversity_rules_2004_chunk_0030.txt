(7) The Authority shall stipulate that benefits shall ensure conservation and sustainable use of biological diversity. 
 
(8) Where biological resources or knowledge is accessed form a specific individual or a group of individuals or organizations, the Authority may take steps to ensure that the agreed amount is paid directly to them through the district administration. The Authority shall monitor the flow of benefits as determined under sub rule (4) in a manner determined by it.
