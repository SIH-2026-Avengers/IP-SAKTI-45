CHAPTER XII 
PERFORMERS' SOCIETY 
68. Registration and management of Performers' society.-(1) For the purpose of carrying on the business of issuing or granting licences in respect of performers' rights under sub-section (1) and proviso to sub-section (2) of section 38A and section 39A, there shall be a separate performers' society for each class of performers such as actors, singers, musicians, dancers, acrobats, jugglers, conjurers, snake charmers, persons delivering lectures or any other group of persons who can make a performance; 
Provided that the Central Government may allow registration of a society for performers' of different classes of performers' in cases where the performances are inter-connected or closely related to each other. 
(2) Such society shall be called as Performers' Society of that specific category.
