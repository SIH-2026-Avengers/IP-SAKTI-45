CHAPTER X 
LICENCE FOR PUBLICATION, TRANSLATION AND RE-PRODUCTION OF WORK 
 38. Application for license.-(1) An application for a licence under sub-section 
(1A) of section 32 and section 32A to publish or to translate any work in any language or to reproduce any published work shall be made in Form VII and shall be accompanied by the fee specified in the Second Schedule. 
(2) Every such application shall be in respect on one work only and in respect of translation of a work into one language only. 
39. Notice of application.-(1) A copy of such application shall be served by registered post on the owner of copyright. 
(2) The Board shall give an opportunity of being to the applicant and also, wherever practicable, to any person claiming any interest in the copyright of the work, and may take such evidence in respect of the application, as it may deem fit.
