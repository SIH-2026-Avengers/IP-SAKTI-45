CHAPTER XV 
MAKING OR ADAPTING THE WORK BY ORGANIZATIONS WORKING FOR THE 
BENEFIT OF PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES 
 
76. Maintenance of records. - The organization working for the benefit of the persons with disabilities and intending to adapt, reproduce, issue of copies or communication to public of any work in any accessible format shall maintain at its principal place of business a register containing the following details, namely:- 
(a) the name of the work, author or owner or publisher along with year of publication; 
(b) the details of the accessible format in which the work is produced; 
(c) the nature of activity such as adaptation, reproduction, issue of copies or communication to the public; 
(d) total number of copies made, list of persons with disabilities to whom copies have been distributed; and 
(e) price, if any, charged on the copies.
