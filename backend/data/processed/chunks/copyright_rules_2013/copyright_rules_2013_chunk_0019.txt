12. Manner of determining royalties.- The Board shall determine the amount of royalty to be deposited by the applicant. The Board may while determining the royalty shall take into consideration the following- 
(a) the prevailing standards of royalties with regard to such works; and 
(b) such other matters as may be considered relevant by the Board. 
13. Extension of the period of licence.- The Board may, on the application of the licensee, if it is satisfied that the licensee was for sufficient reasons unable to produce and publish the translation or reproduce the work or communicate the work to the public within the period specified in the licence, extend such period.
