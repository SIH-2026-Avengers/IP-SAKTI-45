3. Preparation.-The stock seed virus which has been establish ed as pure, safe and immunogenic in avian species shall be used for preparing the seed virus for vaccine production. 
(i) 
 Safety Test.-The stock seed virus shall be non-pathogenic for chickens as determined by the following procedure: 
The groups of at least 25 chickens each at one day of age shall be used. These chickens shall be of the same source and batch, be susceptible to Marek's disease and be kept in isolated group. 
Group I : Each chicken shall be injected with 0.2 ml. of 10 times as much viable virus as will be contained in one dose of vaccine by intramuscular route.
