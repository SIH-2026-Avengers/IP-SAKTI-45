6.3 Trituration: - Trituration technique is used to manufacture drugs from insoluble strains. The procedure / method specified in the Homoeopathic pharmacopoeia shall be adopted. 
6.4 Formulations:- Compound formulations shall preferably be in liquid and solid forms and the potency of the ingredients shall be in detectable quantity preferably be in

(a) Pellets shall be manufactured in clean rooms free from particulate contaminants.
