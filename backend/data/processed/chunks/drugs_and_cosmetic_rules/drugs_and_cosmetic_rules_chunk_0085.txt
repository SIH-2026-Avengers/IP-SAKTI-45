62. Sale at more than one place. - If drugs are sold or stocked for sale at more than one place, separate application shall be made, and a separate licence shall be issued, in respect of each such place: 
Provided that this shall not apply to itinerant vendors who have no specified place of business and who will be licensed to conduct business in a particular area within the jurisdiction of the licensing authority. 
62-A. Restricted licences in Forms 20-A and 21-A. - (a) Restricted licences in Forms 
20-A and 21-A shall be issued, subject to the discretion of the licensing authority, to dealers or persons in respect of drugs whose sale does not require the supervision of a qualified person.
