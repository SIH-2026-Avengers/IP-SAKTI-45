61. Forms of licences to sell drugs. - (1) A licence to sell, stock, exhibit or offer for sale or distribute drugs other than those specified in Schedules C, C(1) and X and by retail or restricted licence or by wholesale, shall be issued in Form 20, Form 20-A or Form 20-B, as the case may be: 
Provided that a licence in Form 20-A shall be valid for only such drugs as are specified in the licence. 
(2) A licence to sell, stock, exhibit for sale or offer for sale or distribute drugs specified in 
Schedules C and C(1) excluding those specified in Schedule X, by retail or restricted licence or by wholesale shall be issued in Form 21, Form 21-A or Form 21-B, as the case may be: 
Schedule C and shall be valid for only such Schedule C(1) drugs as are specified in the licence. 
(3) A licence to sell, stock or exhibit for sale or offer for sale or distribute drugs specified in 
Schedule X by retail or by wholesale shall be issued in Form 20-F or Form 20-G, as the case may be.
