3. Production area.- 
 
3.1. The production area shall be designed to allow the production preferably in uni-flow and with logical sequence of operations. 
 
3.2. In order to avoid the risk of cross-contamination, separate dedicated and self-contained facilities shall be made available for the production of sensitive pharmaceutical products like penicillin or biological preparations with live micro-organisms. Separate dedicated facilities shall be provided for the manufacture of contamination causing and potent products such as 
Beta lactum, Sex Hormones and Cyto-toxic substances.
