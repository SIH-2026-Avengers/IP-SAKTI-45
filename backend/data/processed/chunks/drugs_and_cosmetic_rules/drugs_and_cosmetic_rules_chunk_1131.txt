1. Synonym - Milk Ring Test Antigen. 
 
2. Definition - The antigen is a suspension of pure growth culture of standard strain of 
Brucella abortus strain 99 strained supravitally with 2,3,5, triphenyl tetrazolium chloride suspended in 0.85 per cent saline containing 1 per cent glycerol and 1per cent phenol. 
3. Preparation - Smooth strain of Brucella abortus strain 99 is grown on potato infusion agar for 48 to 72 hours in Roux flasks at 370C. Condensation fluid if any is pipetted off before washing. Each flask is washed with about 20 ml. of normal saline. The pooled washing is filtered through a gauze and the filtrate is collected in a measuring cylinder. To every 500 ml. of the filtrate 1 g. of 2,3,5 triphenyl tetrazolium chloride is added immediately. The container is shaken for thirty minutes till the tetrazolium salt is dissolved.
