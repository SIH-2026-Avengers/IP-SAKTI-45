3. Preparation.-The above strains are grown separately in suitable liquid media under conditions which ensure maximum toxin production. The cultures are checked for purity and toxicity in mice. Solution of Formaldehyde I.P., of analytical grade is added to a 0.5 per cent final concentration and formalised cultures are kept at 37º C till the product is sterilised and atoxic. 
The formalised anacultures are pooled, precipitated by the addition of Aluminium Chloride , 20 per cent solution in distilled water to have a final concerntration of the chemical to 10 per cent and pH adjusted to 6.0. The sedimented toxoid is reconstituted to half its the original volume in normal saline. 
4. Standards : - 
(a) Description.-It is a whitish liquid when shaken thoroughly to contain killed bacteria and toxoid in suspension.
