3. Proper Name.-The proper name of any vaccine shall be the name of the micro-organism from which it is made followed by the word "Vaccine" unless the Schedule otherwise provides or if there is no other special provision in this Schedule, some other name as approved by the licensing authority: 
 
Provided that in the case of the under-mentioned preparations the proper name of the vaccine may be as follows : 
1. 
Anthrax Spore Vaccine (Living).
