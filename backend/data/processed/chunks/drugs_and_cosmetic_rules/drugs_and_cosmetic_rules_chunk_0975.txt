8. 
Labelling.-The provisions of "Labelling" as laid down for Bacterial Vaccines shall also apply to Viral Vaccines. The following additional information shall also be included on the label of the outside container. 
(i) 
 The name and percentage of bacteriostatic agent contained in the vaccine. 
(ii) If the vaccine as issued for sale contains any substance other than the diluent, the nature and strength of such substance.
