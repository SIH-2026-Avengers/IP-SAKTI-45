121. Test for freedom from abnormal toxicity.-The tests for freedom from abnormal toxicity shall be carried out as per the current edition of Indian Pharmacopoeia in the case of each batch of the serum tested by the licensee or by an institution approved by the licensing authority for the purpose of carrying out test on its behalf.
