Fluorescence 
 
When viewed under screened ultra - violet light, not more than occasional points of fluorescence are observed. 
 
Packing, Labelling and Storage 
 
Bandage Cloth shall be packed securely so as to allow normal handling sand transport without tearing and exposing the contents. In packages of cut and rolled bandages, each bandage shall also individually be wrapped in a suitable paper. The net content is stated on the label in terms of length and width. Bandage Cloth must be stored in packed condition, protected from dust. The packings of Bandage Cloth shall be labelled prominently with the words "Non - 
Sterile".
