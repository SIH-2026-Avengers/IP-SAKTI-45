4. The licensee shall inform the Licensing Authority in writing in the event of any change in the constitution of the firm operating under the licence. Where any change in the constitution of the firm takes place, the current licence shall be deemed to be valid for a maximum period of three months from the date on which the change takes place unless, in the meantime, a fresh licence has been taken from the Licencing 
Authority in the name of the firm with the changed constitution. 
5. The licensee shall inform the Licencing Authority in writing in the event of any change in ownership of the vehicle specified in this licence within seven days of such change.
