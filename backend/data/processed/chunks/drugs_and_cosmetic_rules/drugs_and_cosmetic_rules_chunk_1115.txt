ANTI-BLACKQUARTER SERUM 
 
1. Synonym - Blackleg Anti-serum, Clostridium Chauvoei-Anti-serum. 
 
2. Definition - Anti-Blackquarter serum is the serum of suitable animals containing the substances that have a specific neutralising effect on Clostridium Chauvoei. 
3. Preparation - It is prepared by injecting subcutaneously or intramuscularly increasing doses of formolised cultures of Cl. Chauvoai into buffalo-bulls.
