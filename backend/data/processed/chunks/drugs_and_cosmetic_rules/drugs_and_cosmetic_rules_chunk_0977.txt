4. Standards : 
(a) 
Description.-Light mauve coloured scales. 
(b) Identification.-When reconstituted vaccine is applied to scarified area of the skin of a fowl it produces characteristics lesions of fowl pox. This product should afford protection against fowl pox. 
(c) 
Moisture Content.-Moisture content in the finished product should not exceed 
1.0 per cent. 
(d) 
Safety test.-For testing each batch of fowl pox vaccine twelve healthy cockerels, or other suitable young chicken each weighing not less than 400 g. from the same source are taken. This group of twelve birds is immunized at least twenty-one days previous to the test, with fowl pox vaccine.
