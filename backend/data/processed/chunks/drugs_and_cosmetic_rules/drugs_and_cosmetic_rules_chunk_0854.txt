M. Labels: 
The labels on every blood bag containing blood and/or component shall contain the following particulars, namely : 
(1) The proper name of the product in a prominent place and in bold letters on the bag. 
(2) Name and address of the blood bank. 
(3) Licence number. 
(4) Serial number. 
(5) 
The date on which the blood is drawn and the date of expiry as prescribed under Schedule 
P to these rules. 
 
(6) 
A coloured label shall be put on every bag containing blood. The following colour scheme for the said labels shall be used for different groups of blood.
