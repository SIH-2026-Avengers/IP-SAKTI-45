157A. Maintaining of records of raw material used by licensed manufacturing unit of 
Ayurveda, Siddha and Unani drugs in the preceding financial year. Each licensed manufacturing unit of Ayurveda or Siddha or Unani drugs shall keep a record of raw material used by each licensed manufacturing unit of Ayurveda, Siddha or Unani drugs as the case may be in the proforma given in Schedule TA in respect of all raw materials utilized by that unit in the manufacture of Ayurveda or Siddha or Unani drugs in the preceding financial year, and shall submit the same by the 30th day of June of the succeeding financial year to the State Drug 
Licensing Authority of Ayurveda, Siddha and Unani drugs and to the National Medicinal Plants 
Board or any agency nominated by the National Medicinal Plant Board for this purpose.
