3.6. We shall from time to time report for any administrative action take due to adverse reaction, viz., market withdrawal regulatory restriction, or cancellation of authorization and/or "not of standard quality report" of any drug pertaining to the Registration 
Certificate declared by any Regulatory Authority of any country where the drug is marketed/sold or distributed. The despatch and marketing of the drug in such cases, shall be stopped immediately and the licensing authority shall be informed immediately.
