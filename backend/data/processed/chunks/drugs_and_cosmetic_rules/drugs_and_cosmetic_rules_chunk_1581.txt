4. Storage Area.-The licensee shall provide separate storage facilities for quarantine and sterilized products. 
 
An a are not less than 10 square metres shall be provided for each of them. 
 
5. Washing, drying and sealing area. - The licensee shall provide wherever required adequate equipments like water distillation still, deionizer washing machine, Drying Oven with trays for washing, drying and sealing of medical device. 
 
An area not less than 10 square meters shall be provided.
