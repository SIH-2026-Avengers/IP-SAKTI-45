4. Standards : 
 
(a) Description. - It is a dry product, pinkish cream material, readily dispersible in water or a suitable solvent. 
(b) Identification. - It infects CAM of fertile eggs. This is neutralised by canine distemper antiserum. It does not cause distemper after injection into susceptible ferrets or dogs but immunizes them against the disease. 
(c) Moisture content. - Moisture content in the finished product shall not exceed more than 1.0 per cent.
