Immunogenicity Test.-Thirteen susceptible dogs 8-14 weeks old, shall be used for the test 
(ten vaccinates and 3 controls). Blood samples are drawn from these animals and individual sample is tested for antibodies against canine distemper. Ten dogs shall be injected with a predetermined quantity of the virus and remaining 3 dogs are used as unvaccinated controls.
