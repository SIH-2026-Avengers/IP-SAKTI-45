 The applicant shall provide adequate space, plant and equipment for any or all the operations of blood collection or blood processing. The space, plant and equipment required for various operations is given in Schedule 'F', Part XII-B and/or XII-C or Part XIID.

 The applicant shall provide adequate arrangements for storage of whole human blood, human blood components and blood products. 
(v) 
The applicant shall furnish to the Licensing Authority, if required to do so, data on the stability of whole human blood, its components or blood products which are likely to deteriorate, for fixing the date of expiry which shall be printed on the labels of such products on the basis of the data so furnished.
