62-B. Conditions to be satisfied before a licence in Form 20-A or Form 21-A is granted. (1) A licence in Form 20-A or Form 21-A shall not be granted to any person, unless the authority empowered to grant the licence is satisfied that the premises in respect of which the licence is to be granted are adequate and equipped with proper storage accommodation for preserving the properties of drugs to which the licence applies: 
Provided that this condition shall not apply in the case of licence granted to itinerant vendors.
