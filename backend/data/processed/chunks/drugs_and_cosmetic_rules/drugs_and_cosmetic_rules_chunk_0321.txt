(9) If the Ethics Committee fails to comply with any of the conditions of registration, the 
Licensing Authority may, after giving an opportunity to show cause why such an order should not be passed, by an order in writing stating the reasons therefor, suspend or cancel the registration of the Ethics Committee for such period as considered necessary.
