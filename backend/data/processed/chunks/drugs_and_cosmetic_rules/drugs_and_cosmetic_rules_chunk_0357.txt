124-B. Standards for patent or proprietary medicines.- The standards for patent or proprietary medicines shall be those laid down in Schedule V and such medicines shall also comply with the standards laid down in the Second Schedule to the Act. 
124-C. Standards for Surgical Dressings.-The standards for Surgical Dressings shall be such as are laid down in Schedule F(II). 
124-D. Standards for Sterilised Umbilical tapes.-The standards for Sterilised Umbilical tapes shall be as laid down in Schedule F(III).
