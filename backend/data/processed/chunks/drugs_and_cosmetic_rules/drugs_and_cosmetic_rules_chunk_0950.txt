FOWL CHOLERA VACCINE (POLYVALENT) 
 
1. Synonym.-Pasteurella Septica Vaccine (Avian). 
 
2. Definition.-Fowl Cholera Vaccine is a formolised pure broth culture of virulent strains of Pasteurella Septica (Avian). 
3. Preparation.-The strains are grown separately in nutrient broth for 48 hours at 37º C. 
The pure growth is killed by the addition of a Solution of Formaldehyde I.P. in a suitable concentration. The cultures are then mixed in equal proportions and the final vaccine is bottled in suitable containers. 
4. Standard : 
 
(a) Description.-It is light yellow liquid containing dead bacteria in suspension. 
(b) Identification.-It protects susceptible birds against P. aviseptica infection.
