ANTI-SERA AND THEIR GENERAL STANDARD 
 
Anti-sera contain the immune substances that have a specific prophylactic or therapeutic action when injected into animals exposed to or suffering from a disease due to a specific micro- organism or its toxin. Anti-sera are classified into three groups. 
(i)Antitoxic sera (Antitoxin) 
(ii)Antibacterial sera 
(iii)Antiviral sera
