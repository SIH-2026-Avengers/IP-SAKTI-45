4. Standards : - 
(a) Description.-It is a whitish liquid when shaken thoroughly to contain killed bacteria and toxoid in suspension. 
(b) Identification.-When injected into susceptible animals it stimulates the production of epsilon and beta antitoxins against Cl. perfringens type D and C and also antitoxins against Cl. septicum and toxin of Cl. oedematiens.
