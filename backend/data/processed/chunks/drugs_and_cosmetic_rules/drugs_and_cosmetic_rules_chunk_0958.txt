4. Standard.- 
 
(a) Description.-It is an opalescent liquid containing dead bacteria in suspension. 
 
(b) Identification.-It protects susceptible animals against infection with Sal. abortus equi. 
(c) Sterility Test.-Complies with th e tests for sterility described in the gener al monograph on "Bacterial Vaccines". 
(d) Safety Test.-Six white mice each weighing not less than 18 g. are inoculated intraperitoneally each with 0.5 ml. of the product. None of the mice should die of salmonellosis. 
The mice are observed for ninety-six hours. 
5. 
Labelling and Storage.-Should comply with the requirements of "Labelling" and 
"Storage" as laid down in the general monograph on "Bacterial Vaccines". 
 
6. Expiry Date.-The date of expiry of potency of the vaccine shall be not more than six months from the date of manufacture. 
STREPTOCOCCUS EQUI VACCINE
