AVIAN ENCEPHALOMYELITIS VACCINE (LIVING) 
 
1. Synonyms.- Avian Encephalomyelitis Vaccine Freeze dried. 
 
2. Definition.-A virus bearing tissue and fluid suspension from embryonated hen's eggs. 
 
3. Preparation.-The stock seed virus which has been establish ed as pure, safe and immunogenic shall be used for preparing the vaccine. 
(i) Each lot of stock seed virus shall be tested for pathogenicity by chicken embryo inoculation test : 
 
(a) One dose of the seed lot shall be mixed with 9 volumes of sterile heat inactivated specific, antiserum to neutralise vaccine virus in the product.
