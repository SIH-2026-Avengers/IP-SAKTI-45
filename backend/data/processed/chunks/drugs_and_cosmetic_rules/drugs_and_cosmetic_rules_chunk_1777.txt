Note 1. - Patent or proprietary medicines containing vitamins intended for prophylactic, therapeutic or paediatric use shall bear on the label the words "For Prophylactic Use", 
"For Therapeutic Use" or "For Paediatric Use" as the case may be. In the cases of paediatric preparations the age of the infant or the child for whose use it is intended, shall be given in addition to the particulars re quired to be given under these rules.
