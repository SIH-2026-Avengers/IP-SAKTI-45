For preparing the polyvalent vaccine, equal number of brains collected from mice infected with different types of the virus are homogeinised with 5-10 times its volume of sterile lactose buffer medium (pH 7.2) containing antibiotics. The suspension is centrifuged at 1500 r.p.m. for five minutes. The supernatant liquid is distributed in ampoules in suitable quantities and freeze dried.
