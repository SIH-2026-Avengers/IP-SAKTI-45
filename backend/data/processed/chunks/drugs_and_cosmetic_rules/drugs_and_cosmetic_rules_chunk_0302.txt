122-C. * * * 
122-D. Permission to import or manufacture fixed dose combination.- (1) An application for permission to import or manufacture fixed dose combination of two or more drugs as defined in clause (c) Rule 122-E shall be made to the Licensing Authority as defined in clause (b) of Rule 21 in Form 44, accompanied by a fee of fifteen thousand rupees and shall be accompanied by such information and data as is required in Appendix VI of Schedule Y.
