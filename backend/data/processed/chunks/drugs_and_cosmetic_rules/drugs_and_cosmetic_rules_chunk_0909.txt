C. PERSONNEL 
Cord blood bank shall have following categories of whole time competent technical staff, namely:- 
1. Medical Director:- The operation of cord blood bank shall be conducted under the active directions and supervision of a Medical Director who is a whole time employee and is possessing a Post Graduate degree in medicine - MD [Pathology/Transfusion 
Medicine/Microbiology] and has experience / training in cord blood processing and 
Cryogenic Storage.
