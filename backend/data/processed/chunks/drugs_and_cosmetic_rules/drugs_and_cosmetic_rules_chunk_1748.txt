24. For Hypodermic tablets and ophthalmic preparations, which are required to be manufactured under aseptic conditions, records shall be maintained indicating the precautions taken during the process of manufacture to ensure that aseptic conditions are maintained. 
B. Parenteral preparations 
1. 
Serial Number. 
2. 
Name of the product. 
3. 
Reference of the master formula record. 
4. 
Batch/Lot size. 
5. 
Batch No. and/or Lot No. 
6. 
Date of commencement of manufacture and date of completion. 
7. Names of all ingredients, specifications and quantity required for the Lot/Batch size and quantity actually used. All weighing and measurements shall be carried out by a responsible person and initialled by him and shall be counter-signed by the technical staff under whose personal supervision the stocks are issued and by another competent technical staff under whose supervision the ingredients are used for manufacture. 
8. 
Control numbers of raw materials used in the formulation.
