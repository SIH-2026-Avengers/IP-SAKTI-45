II. REQUIREMENTS FOR MANUFACTURE OF BLOOD PRODUCTS FROM BULK 
FINISHED PRODUCTS 
Where the blood products, such as Albumin, Plasma Protein Fraction, Immunoglobulins and 
Coagulation Factor Concentrates are manufactured through the manufacturing activities of filling and sealing the blood products from bulk powder or solution or both, the requirements as they apply to the manufacture blood products from whole blood shall apply mutatis mutandis to such manufacture of blood products, unless other requirements have been approved by the Central Licence Approving Authority.]
