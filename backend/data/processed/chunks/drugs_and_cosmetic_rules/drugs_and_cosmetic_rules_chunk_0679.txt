Conditions of Licence 
1. The licence and the certificate of renewal in force shall be kept at the approved premises and shall be produced at the request of the Inspector appointed under the 
Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940. 
2. If the licensee wishes to undertake during the currency of the licence the manufacture of any drug specified in Schedule X not included above, he should apply to the Licensing 
Authority for the necessary endorsement as provided in Rule 75(4). This licence will be deemed to be applicable to the items so endorsed. 
3. Any change in the expert staff shall be forthwith reported to the Licensing Authority.
