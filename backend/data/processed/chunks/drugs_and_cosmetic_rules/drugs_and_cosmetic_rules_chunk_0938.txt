5. Labelling and Storage.-Should comply with the requirements for "Labelling" and 
"Storage" as laid down in the general monograph on "Bacterial Vaccines". 
[6. Expiry date.-The date of expiry of the potency of the vaccine shall be not more than two years from the date of manufacture if stored in 40C and six months, if stored at room temperature.]
