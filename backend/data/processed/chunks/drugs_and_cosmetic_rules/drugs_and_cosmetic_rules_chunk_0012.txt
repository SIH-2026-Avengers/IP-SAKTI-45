PART IV 
[IMPORTAND REGISTRATION] 
21. In this Part- 
(a) 'Import licence' means either a licence in Form 10 to import drugs [***], excluding those specified in Schedule X, or a licence in Form 10-A to import drugs specified in 
Schedule X; 
(b) 
"licensing authority" means the authority appointed by the Central Government to perform the duties of the licensing authority under these Rules and includes any person to whom the power of a licensing authority may be delegated under Rule 22; 
(c) 
"licence for examination, test or analysis" means a licence in Form 11 to import small quantities of drugs, the import of which is otherwise prohibited, for the purpose of examination, test or analysis.
