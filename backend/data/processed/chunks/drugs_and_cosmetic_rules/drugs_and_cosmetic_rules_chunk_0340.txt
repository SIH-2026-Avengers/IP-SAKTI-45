122-K. Further application after rejection.- If within a period of six months from the rejection of application for a licence the applicant informs the Licensing Authority that the conditions laid down have been satisfied and deposits an inspection fee of fees of rupees two hundred and fifty] the Licensing Authority may, if after causing further inspection to be made is satisfied that the conditions for the grant or renewal of a licence have been complied with, shall grant or renew the licence in Form 28C or Form 28E or Form 28-F;
