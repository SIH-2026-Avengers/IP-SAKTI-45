C. PERSONNEL : 
Every blood bank shall have following categories of whole time competent technical staff : 
(a) Medical Officer, possessing the qualifications specified in condition (i) of rule 
122-G. 
 
(b) Blood Bank Technician (s), possessing - 
 
(i) Degree in Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT) with six month's experience in the testing of blood and/or its components; or 
 
(ii) Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT) with one year 's experience in the testing of blood and/or its components, the degree or diploma being from a University/Institution recognised by the Central Gover nment or State 
Government. 
 
(c) Registered Nurse (s). 
 
(d) Technical Supervisor (where blood components are manufactured), possessing 
 
(i) Degree in Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT) with six month's experience in the preparation of blood components; or
