3. 
No sale of any drug shall be made to a person not holding the requisite licence to sell, stock or exhibit for sale or distribute the drug: Provided that this condition shall not apply to the sale of any drug to : (a) an officer or authority purchasing on behalf of 
Government, or (b) a hospital, medical, educational or research institution or a 
Homoeopathic medical practitioner for the purpose of supply to his patients.] 
[4. The licensee shall inform the Licensing Authority in writing in the event of any change in the constitution of the firm operating under the licence and the current licence shall be valid only for a period of three months from the date on which the change takes place unless, in the meantime, a fresh licence has been taken from the 
Licensing Authority in the name of the firm with the changed constitution.]
