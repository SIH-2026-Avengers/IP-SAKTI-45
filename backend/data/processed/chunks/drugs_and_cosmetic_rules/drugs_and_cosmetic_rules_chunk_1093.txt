A lot of seed virus shall be mixed with 9 volumes of sterile, heat-inactivated specific antiserum to neutralise and the vaccine virus serum mixture shall be inoculated into each of at least 20 fully susceptible chicken embryos of 9 - 11 days old (0.1 ml. on CAM and 0.1 ml. in the allantoic sac). Eggs are candled daily for 7 days. Deaths occurring during first 24 hours shall be discarded but at least 18 viable embryos shall survive 24 hours post inoculation for a valid test.
