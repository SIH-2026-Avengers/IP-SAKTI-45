3. Proper Name - The proper name of any diagnostic agent is the name of micro-organism from which it is made, followed by the word 'antigen' unless the Schedule otherwise provides, or, it may be derived from the name of the organism responsible for the causation of the disease or if there is no special provision in the Schedule, the name approved by the licensing authority. In the case of the undermentioned preparations the proper name of the diagnostic agent may be as follows :- 
1. Abortus Bang Ring (A.B.R.) Antigen 
 
2. Brucella Abortus Coloured Antigen 
 
3. Brucella Abortus Plain Antigen 
 
4. Johnin 
 
5. Mallein 
 
6. Salmonella Abortus Equi. "H" Antigen 
 
7. Salmonella Pullorum Coloured Antigen 
 
8. Salmonella Pullorum Plain Antigen 
 
9. Tuberculin.
