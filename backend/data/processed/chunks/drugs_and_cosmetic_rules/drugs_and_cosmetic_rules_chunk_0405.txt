147. Exemption of cosmetics not manufactured for consumption or sale in India from the provisions of this Part.- Labels on packages or containers of cosmetics not manufactured for consumption or sale in India shall be adapted to meet the specific requirements, if any, of the consignee: 
Provided that where a cosmetic is required by the consignee to be not labelled with the name and address of the manufacturer, the labels on packages or containers shall bear a code number as approved by the Licensing Authority mentioned in Rule 21. 
148. Manner of labelling.-Subject to other provisions of the rules, a cosmetic shall carry-(1) on both the inner and outer labels: 
(a) 
 the name of the cosmetics, 
(b) the name of the manufacturer and complete address of the premises of the manufacturer where the cosmetic has been manufactured. cc) use before ................ month and year)
