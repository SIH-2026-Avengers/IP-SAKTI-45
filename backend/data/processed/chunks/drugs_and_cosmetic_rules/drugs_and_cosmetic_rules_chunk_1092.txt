AVIAN INFECTIOUS BRONCHITIS VACCINE (LIVING) 
 
1. Synonyms. - Avian Infectious Bronchitis Vaccine (Living) freeze dried. 
 
2. Definition. - It is freeze dried product of low virulent Avian Infectious Bronchitis Virus grown in embryonated hen's eggs of cultivated in cell culture.
