RINDERPEST LAPINISED VACCINE (LIVING) 
1. 
Synonym. - Rabbit Adapted Cattle Plague Vaccine (Living) Lapinised Vaccine (Living). 
2. Definition. - Rinderpest Lapinised Vaccine is a suspension of a modified living virus 
(e.g. Nakamura III Strain) prepared with the blood spleen and mesenteric lymph glands of infected rabbits and is freeze dried.
