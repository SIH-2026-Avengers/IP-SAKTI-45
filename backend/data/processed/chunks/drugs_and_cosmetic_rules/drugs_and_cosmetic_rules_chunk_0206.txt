78-A. Conditions of licence in Form 28A or Form 28DA. (1) The licence in 
Form 28A or Form 28DA shall be deemed to be cancelled or suspended, if the licence owned by the licensee in Form 28 or Form 28D and Form 28D whose manufacturing facilities have been availed of by the licensee is cancelled or suspended, as the case may be, under these rules. 
(2) The Licensee shall comply with the provisions of the Act, and of these rules and with such further requirements if any, as may be specified in any rules subsequently made under Chapter 
IV of the Act, provided that where such further requirements are specified in the rules, those would come into force four months after publication in the Official Gazette.
