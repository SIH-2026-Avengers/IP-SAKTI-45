(i) 
 No drug shall be sold unless such drug is purchased under a cash or credit memo from a duly licensed dealer or a duly licensed manufacturer. 
(ii) No sale of any drug shall be made for purpose of resale to a person not holding the requisite licence to sell, stock or exhibit for sale or distribute the drug: 
Provided that this condition shall not apply to the sale of any drug to - 
(a) an officer or authority purchasing on behalf of Government, or 
(b) a hospital, medical, educational or research institution or a registered medical practitioner for the purpose of supply to his patients, or 
(c) a manufacturer of hydrogenated vegetable oils, beverages, confectionery and other non-medicinal products, where such drugs are required for processing these products.
