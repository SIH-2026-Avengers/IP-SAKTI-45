36. Imports of drugs for personal use. - Small quantities of drugs, the imports of which is otherwise prohibited under Section 10 of the Act, may be imported for personal use subject to the following conditions - 
(i) the drugs shall form part of a passenger 's bona fide baggage and shall be the property of, and be intended for, the exclusive personal use of the passenger; 
(ii) 
 the drugs shall be declared to the Customs Authorities if they so direct; 
(iii)
