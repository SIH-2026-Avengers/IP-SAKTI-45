1.1. (I) Medical Services: 
The Manufacturer shall also provide: - 
(a) Adequate facilities for first aid; 
(b) Medical examination of workers at the time of employment and periodical check up thereafter by a physician once a year, with particular attention being devoted to freedom from infections. 
Records thereof shall be maintained.
