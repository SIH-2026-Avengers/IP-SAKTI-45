Conditions of licence 
1. 
The licence and any certificate of renewal in force shall be kept on the approved premises and shall be produced at the request of an Inspector, appointed under the 
Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940. 
2. Any change in the technical staff names in the licence shall be reported forthwith to the licensing authority. 
3. This licence shall be deemed to extend to such additional items as the licensee may intimate to the licensing authority from time to time, and as may be endorsed by the licensing authority.
