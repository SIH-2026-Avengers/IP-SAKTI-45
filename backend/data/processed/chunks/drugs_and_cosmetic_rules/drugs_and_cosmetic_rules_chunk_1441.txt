2. Sifting, mixing and granulation- 
 
2.1. Unless operated as a closed system, mixing, sifting and blending equipments shall be fitted with dust extractors 73B[or in a dedicated area for each operation] 
2.2. Residues from sieving operations shall be examined periodically for evidence of the presence of unwanted materials.
