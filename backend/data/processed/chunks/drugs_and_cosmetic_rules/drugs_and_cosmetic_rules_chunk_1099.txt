ANTISERA

SERA FROM LIVING ANIMALS 
1. Definitions - (i) This part of the Schedule applies to anti-bacterial sera, antiviral sera and anti-toxic sera which are prepared by injecting bacteria or viruses or their products into buffalo-bulls or other suitable animals so as to produce active immunity which is manifested by the formation of antibody.
