(i) Mallein subcutaneous.- Two ponies, previously sensitised with A. Mallei and controls, are injected each with 1 ml. of the product subcutaneously in the neek region. The animals are observed for local reaction and rise in temperature. Local reaction is manifested by a hot tense, painful swelling becoming prominent within twenty-four hours. The rise in temperature is observed by recording the body temperature at the time of inoculation and subsequently at short intervals. A rise in temperature of 10C or more above normal is indicative of infection.
