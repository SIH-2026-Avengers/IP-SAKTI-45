(e) Safety Test.. - Ten healthy susceptible chickens 5 - 10 days from the same source, batch shall be vaccinated with ten field doses of the vaccine and along with five chicks from same batch as unvaccinated controls by the prescribed route and observed 7 or 21 days post vaccination.
