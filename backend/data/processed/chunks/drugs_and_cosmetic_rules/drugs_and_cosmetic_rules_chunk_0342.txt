(i) (a) 
The licensee shall provide and maintain adequate staff, plant and premises for the proper operation of a Blood Bank for processing whole human blood, its components and/or manufacture of blood products. 
(b) 
The licensee shall maintain staff, premises and equipment as specified in Rule 122- 
G. The licensee shall maintain necessary records and registers as specified in 
Schedule F, Parts XII-B and XII-C. 
(c) 
The licensee shall test in his own laboratory whole human blood, its components and blood products and [maintain records and] registers in respect of such tests as specified in Schedule F, Parts XII-B and XII-C or Part XII D. The records and registers shall be maintained for a period of five years from the date of manufacture.
