(2) Before approval in form 48 is granted or renewed, the following conditions shall be complied with by the applicants, namely:- 
(i) 
 The premises where the tests are carried out shall be well lighted and properly ventilated except where the nature of tests of any Ayurvedic, Siddha and Unani drugs warrants otherwise. Wherever necessary, the premises shall be air - conditioned so as to maintain the accuracy and functioning of laboratory instruments or to enable the performance of special tests such as sterility tests and microbiological tests. 
(ii) (a) The applicant shall provide adequate space having regard to the nature and number of samples of drugs proposed to be tested:
