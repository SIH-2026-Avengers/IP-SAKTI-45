5.6. The footwear shall be of suitable plastic material or rubber material and shall be daily cleaned with a bactericide. 
5.7. Safety goggles or numbered glasses with side extensions shall be used inside aseptic areas. 
These shall be sanitised by a suitable method. 
5.8. Garment changing procedure shall be documented and operators trained in this aspects. A full size mirror shall be provided in the final change room for the operator to verify that he is appropriately attired in the garments. Periodic inspection of the garments shall be done by responsible staff. 
6. Sanitation- 
 
6.1. There shall be written procedures for the sanitation of sterile processing facilities. Employees carrying out sanitation of aseptic areas shall be trained specifically for this purpose.
