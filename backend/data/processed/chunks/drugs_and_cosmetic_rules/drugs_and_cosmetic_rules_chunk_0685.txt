4. The licensee shall inform the Licensing Authority and/or Central Licence Approving 
Authority in writing in the event of any change in the constitution of the firm operating under the licence. Where any change in the constitution of the firm takes place, the current licence shall be deemed to be valid for maximum period of the three months from the date on which the change has taken place unless, in the meantime, a fresh licence has been taken from the Licensing Authority and/or Central Licence Approving 
Authority in the name of the firm with the changed constitution.]
