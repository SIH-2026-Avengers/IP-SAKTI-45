12[FOOT AND MOUTH DISEASE VACCINE (INACTIVATED) 
1 Synonym.-Inactivated Tissue culture mono- or polyvalent Foot and Mouth Disease 
Vaccine. 
2 Definition.-Foot and Mouth Disease Vaccine is a liquid product or preparation containing one or more types of foot and mouth disease virus which have been inactivated in such a way that its immunogenic property is maintained. It may also contain an adjuvant. The vaccine is discribed as monovalent, bivalent, trivalent or polyvalent depending on the number of types of virus used.
