H. REFERENCE SAMPLES 
1. At least two reference samples shall be collected from cord blood unit product prior to cryopreservation and stored at minus 196ºC and shall not be warmer than minus 
150ºC. 
2. At least one additional reference sample shall be stored at minus 76ºC or colder for the purposes other than viability analysis.
