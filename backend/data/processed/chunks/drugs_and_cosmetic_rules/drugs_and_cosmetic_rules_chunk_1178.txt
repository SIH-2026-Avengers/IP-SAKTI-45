For samples less than 15 m. in length, take pieces in such manner that the total area of the selected pieces is not less than 20 per cent of the total area of the sample. 
 
For cut bandages, pieces of 50 cm. in length, cut from 5 different cut bandages in a packing should be taken and weight calculated as an average of 5 readings. 
 
Absorbency :- Take a glass trough of approximate size length 30cm. X width 30 cm. X depth 25 cm. with straight thick walls and flat bottom. Fill it almost full with distilled water leaving only about 5 cm. from the top rim of trough. Maintain the water at 270C ± 10C.
