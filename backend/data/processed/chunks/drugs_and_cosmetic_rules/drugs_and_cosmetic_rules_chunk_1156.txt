SALMONELLA PULLORUM PLAIN ANTIGEN 
1. Synonym - Bacillary White Diarrhoes (B.W.D.) Plain Antigens. 
2. Definition - The antigen is a suspension of pure smooth culture of Salmonella Pullorum in phenol saline. 
3. Preparation - Forty-eight hours old pure culture of smooth strain of S. Pollorum is washed with 0.5 per cent phenol saline and the pooled suspension is adjusted to contain approximately 800 million organisms per ml. by the addition of more carbol saline. The suspension is kept at room temperature for twenty-four hours, and dispensed in suitable containers.
