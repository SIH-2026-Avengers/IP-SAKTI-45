Conditions of License 
 
1. Umbilical cord blood specific for an individual will be collected after signing an agreement with the parent(s), whose child's Umbilical cord blood is to be collected, and the cord blood bank. 
2. Umbilical cord blood shall be collected from hospitals, nursing homes,, birthing centers and from any other place where a consenting mother delivers, under the supervision of the qualified Registered Medical Practitioner responsible for the delivery. 
3. The license and any certificate of renewal in force shall be displayed on the approved premises and the original shall be produced at the request of an inspector appointed under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940.
