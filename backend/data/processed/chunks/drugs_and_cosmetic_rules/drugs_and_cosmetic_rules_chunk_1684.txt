1.1. (F) (C) Finished Goods Stores: 
The finished goods transferred from the production area after proper packaging shall be stored in the finished goods stores within an area marked 'Quarantine'. After the quality control laboratory and the experts have checked the correctness of finished goods with reference to its packing/labeling as well as the finished product quality as prescribed, then it will be moved to 
'Approved Finished Goods Stock' area. Only approved finished goods shall be dispatched as per marketing requirements. Distribution records shall be maintained as required. 
If any Ayurvedic, Siddha and Unani drug needs a special storage conditions, finished goods store shall provide necessary environmental requirements.
