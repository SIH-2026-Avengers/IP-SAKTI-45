160H. Application after rejection- If within a period of six months from the rejection of an application for approval, an applicant informs the approving authority that the conditions laid down have been satisfied and deposits inspection fee of two thousand rupees, the approving authority may, if after causing further inspection to be made and after being satisfied that the conditions for grant of approval have been complied with, grant the approval in Form 48. 
160-I . Renewal.- On an application being made for renewal, the approving authority shall, after causing an inspection to be made and if satisfied that the conditions of the approval and the rules made under the Act have been complied with, shall issue a certificate of renewal in Form 49.
