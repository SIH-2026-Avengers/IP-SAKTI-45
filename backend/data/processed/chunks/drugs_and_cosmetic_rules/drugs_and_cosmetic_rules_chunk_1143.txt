2. Preparation - Young culture of selected strain of Mycoparatuberculosis of bovine origin is grown on synthetic medium and incubated at 370C for ten to twelve weeks. Flasks showing lucurient and pure growth are steamed for three hours thereafter kept at room temperature overnight. The contents are filtered through fine meshed sieve. The filtrate is concentrated over a steam bath to one tenth of its original volume and kept in cold storage for fourteen days before being filtered through Seitz filter. The product is dispensed in ampoules and hermetically sealed. 
3. Standard - 
(a) Description - A yellowish brown to brownish liquid. 
(b) Identification - It produces hot, painful and oedematous swelling at the site of inoculation in animals infected with Mayco-paratuberculosis organism. 
(c) Sterility Test. - Should comply with the test for sterility described in the general monograph on 'Diagnostic Antigen'.
