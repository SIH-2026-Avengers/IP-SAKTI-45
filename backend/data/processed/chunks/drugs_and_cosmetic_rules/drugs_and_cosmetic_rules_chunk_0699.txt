4. Any change in the technical staff shall be forthwith reported to the Licensing Authority and / or Central License Approving Authority. 
5. The licensee shall inform the Licensing Authority and / or Central License Approving 
Authority in writing in the event of any change in the constitution of the firm operating under the license. Where any change in the constitution of the firm takes place, the current license shall be deemed to be valid for maximum period of three months from the date on which the change has taken place unless, in the meantime, a fresh license has been taken from the licensing authority and / or Central License Approving Authority in the name of the firm with the changed constitution."
