8 
Penalty 
Any person who intentionally performs any act in contravention of this Act commits an offence, and is liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding 
T$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 5 years or to both. 
PART III - REGISTRATION OF GEOGRAPHICAL 
INDICATIONS 
9 
Application for registration 
(1) 
The application for registration of a geographical indication shall be filed with the Registrar. 
(2) 
Any natural person, competent authority or legal entity carrying on an activity as a producer in the geographical area specified in the application, with respect to the goods specified in the application, as well as groups of such persons shall have the right to file an application.
