Crude raw materials, packing materials, etc. shall be stored and handled in places earmarked for them and shall not be taken inside the manufacturing areas, without quality control approval. Heating, washing, drying, packing and labelling, etc. wherever needed, shall be done in dedicated ancillary areas adjacent to the manufacturing sections concerned. The walls and floorings of manufacturing areas shall be smooth and free from chinks, cracks and crevices and shall be washable. The design of the windows, windowpanes and all fittings shall be such that they will not facilitate accumulation or lodging of dust and other contaminants.
