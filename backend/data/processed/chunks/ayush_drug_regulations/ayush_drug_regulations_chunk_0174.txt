(x) 
Dedicated area shall be provided for sampling of raw material and excipients to avoid contamination and cross-contamination. 
(xi) 
All the raw materials shall be sampled as per written Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) and tested either by the in-house Ayurvedic, Siddha, Sowa-Rigpa and Unani experts (quality control technical person) or by the laboratories approved by the Government and shall be used only on approval after verifying. 
(xii) 
All raw materials will be monitored for fungal, bacterial contamination with a view to minimise such contamination. 
(xiii) 
The rejected raw material should be removed from other raw material store and should be kept in separate areas until it is disposed off; as per written Standard Operating Procedures (SOP). 
(xiv) 
 Procedure of 'first in first out (FIFO)' and 'first expired first out (FEFO)' should be adopted for raw materials. 
(xv) 
Entry to quarantine and recalled or returned goods shall be restricted only to Authorized persons.
