(i) for sub-paragraph 5.1, the following sub-paragraphs shall be substituted, namely: - 
"5.1. Raw Materials 
(1) All incoming materials shall be quarantined immediately after receipt or processing. All materials shall be stored under appropriate conditions and in an orderly fashion to permit batch segregation and stock rotation by a 'first in/first expiry' - 'first-out' principle. All incoming materials shall be checked to ensure that the consignment corresponds to the order placed. 
(2) All incoming materials shall be purchased from approved sources under valid purchase vouchers. 
(3) Authorised staff appointed by the licensee in this behalf, which may include personnel from the 
Quality Control Department, shall examine each consignment on receipt and shall check each container for integrity of package and seal. Damaged containers shall be identified, recorded and segregated.
