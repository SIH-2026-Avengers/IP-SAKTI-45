Plant materials of Agaricaceae, which are perishable shall be used within one week of collection. 
(b) To facilitate proper identification and purity of the material and to exercise proper quality control of the material, the following conditions must be satisfied: 
(i) the raw materials of plant origin shall be as per pharmacopoeia; 
(ii) if the material used is a mould or of the plant families 
Agaricaceae, 
Polyporaceae/Amanitaceae/Boletaceae/Russulaceae, a whole specimen plant/mould shall be available in properly dried form; 
(iii) the materials shall be free from insecticides, fungicides, etc.; 
(iv) fresh herbs shall be stored in open mesh bags and materials in closed containers; 
(v) each consignment of the material shall be accompanied by a statement of the supplier's name; name of the plant with description of the part supplied; the Pharmacopoeial reference, place of collection, date of packaging and weight.";
