1.1(O) Training - 
(i) 
All concerned personnel shall have adequate training in appropriate fields such as Ayush systems, pharmaceutical technology, taxonomic botany, phytochemistry, pharmacognosy, hygiene, microbiology, handling of complaints and related subjects in periodic manner. 
(ii) 
Training records shall be maintained and periodic assessments of the effectiveness of training programmes shall be made.
