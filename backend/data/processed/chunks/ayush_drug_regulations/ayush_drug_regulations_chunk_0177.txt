1.1(G) Working space - 
(i) 
The manufacturing area shall provide adequate space for manufacturing and quality control operations, ensuring the smooth movement of personnel and materials. It shall allow for the orderly placement of equipment and materials to facilitate safe and efficient operations, minimize the risk of mix-ups between different drugs and raw materials, and prevent cross-contamination between products manufactured, stored, or handled within the premises. 
(ii) 
As far as possible unidirectional and logical flow shall be maintained in all operations. 
(iii) 
Man and material entry into the manufacturing area should be separate, well demarcated and with air lock facility, wherever possible. 
(iv) 
The personnel shall have entry into the manufacturing area through cross-over bench, wherever possible. 
(v) 
The manufacturing area shall not be utilized for dining purposes.
