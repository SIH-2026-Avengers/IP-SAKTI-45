THE GAZETTE OF INDIA : EXTRAORDINARY 
 [PART II-SEC. 3(i)] 
 
Note 1:- The section shall have a clean room facility of ISO Class 5 clean room (ISO 14644-1 clean room standard). The section shall be air-conditioned and humidity controlled. Entry to the sections shall be regulated through air-locks with differential air pressures with the air-lock adjacent to the section having higher pressure and the first one through which entry is made with the least pressure. 
Note 2:- Materials shall be passed to the sections through suitable hatches. 
Note 3:- The personnel shall wear sterile clothing including headgear, which shall not shed fibre. 
Note 4:- Washing of phials shall be done in separate areas with proper equipment. Proper facilities shall be provided in the area for washing vessels. 
Note 5:- Separate area shall be provided for packing and labelling.";
