1.1 (L) Distribution Records -Records of Finished Goods transfer, sale and distribution of each batch of Ayurveda, 
Siddha, Sowa-Rigpa and Unani Drugs, shall be maintained in order to facilitate prompt and complete recall of the batch, if necessary. 
1.1 (M) Record of Market Complaints - 
(i) 
Manufacturers shall maintain a register to record all reports of market complaints related to quality control received regarding the products sold in the market.
