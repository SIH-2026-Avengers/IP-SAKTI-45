(vi) 
The manufacture based on the experience and the characteristics of the particular raw material used in 
Ayurveda, Siddha, Sowa-Rigpa and Unani system shall decide the use of appropriate containers which would protect quality of the raw material as well as prevent it from damage due to dampness, microbiological contamination or rodent and insect infestation, etc. All steel containers should be preferably of SS grade 304. 
(vii) 
If certain raw materials require such controlled environmental conditions, the raw materials stores may be sub-divided with proper enclosures to provide such conditions by making suitable cabins. 
(viii) 
While designing such containers, cabins or areas in the raw materials stores, care may be taken to handle the following different categories of raw materials, namely: - 
(1) raw material of metallic and mineral origin; 
(2) raw material from animal sources;
