"4. Product recalls 
Literature inserted inside the product package should indicate the name address of the manufacturing unit or registered office and telephone number for reporting of any adverse drug reaction by physicians or patients. On receipt of such adverse drug event report, it will be the responsibility of the manufacturer to investigate the report and recall the concerned batch of the product from the market if warranted. 
Standard operating procedures (SOP) should be included for storage of recalled Rasaushadhies in a secure segregated area, complying with the requirements specified for storage, till their final disposal.";
