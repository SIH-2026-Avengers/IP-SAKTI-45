Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 33N of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 (23 of 1940), the Central Government, after consultation with the Ayurvedic, Siddha and Unani Drugs Technical Advisory 
Board hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Drugs Rules, 1945, namely: - 
1. Short title and commencement: - (1) These Rules may be called the Drugs (Eleventh Amendment) Rules, 2026. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
 
2. In the Drug Rules, 1945 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule 154, after sub-rule (3) following sub-rule shall be inserted, namely: - 
"(4) A licence issued under sub-rule (1), shall remain valid perpetually." 
3. In the said rules, in rule 154A, after sub-rule (4) following sub-rule shall be inserted, namely:- 
"(5) A licence issued under sub-rule (1), shall remain valid perpetually." 
 4. In the said rules, rule 156 shall be omitted.
