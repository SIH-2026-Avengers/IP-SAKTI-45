Suitable equipment shall be provided as may be needed, considering the nature of work involved. 
When detergents are used to wash containers and closures for primary packing material, suitable procedure shall be prescribed, followed and finally it should be ensured about the total removal of such materials from the containers and closures before use. Plastic containers which are likely to absorb active principles or which are likely to contaminate the contents shall not be used. 
Glass containers used shall be made of neutral glass. The closures and washers used shall be of inert materials which shall not absorb the active principles or contaminate the contents or which may otherwise be likely to cause deterioration of quality. The containers, closures and packing materials shall protect the properties of the medicines.
