(iii) Premises shall be situated in an environment that, when considered together with measures to protect the manufacturing process, presents minimum risk of causing any contamination of materials or products. 
(iv) Premises used for the manufacture of finished products shall be suitably designed and constructed to facilitate good sanitation. 
(v) Premises shall be carefully maintained, and it shall be ensured that repair and maintenance operations do not present any hazard to the quality of products. 
(vi) Premises shall be cleaned, pest controlled and where applicable, disinfected according to detailed written procedures and records shall be maintained. 
(vii) Electrical supply, lighting, temperature, humidity and ventilation shall be appropriate, and they do not adversely affect, directly or indirectly, either the products during their manufacture and storage or the accurate functioning of equipment.
