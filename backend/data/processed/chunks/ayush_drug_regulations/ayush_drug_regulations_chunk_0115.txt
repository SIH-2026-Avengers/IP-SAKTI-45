(a) Rooms - The manufacturing area shall be designed, constructed and maintained to suit the manufacturing of respective sections. The manufacturing of homeopathic medicine should take place in an controlled environment and should be of an appropriate level of cleanliness. The incoming air in the production area shall be filtered through at least five- micron filter. 
Where dust is generated (for eg. during sampling, weighing, mixing and processing operations, packaging of powder), measures shall be taken to avoid cross contamination and facilitate cleaning. The production area shall be cleaned and sanitized at the end of every production process and line clearance from quality control and quality assurance is a must before the next batch is taken into production.
