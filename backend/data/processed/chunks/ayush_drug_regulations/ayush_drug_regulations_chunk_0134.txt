"3.3 Containers and Closures Section. -Separate area for preparation of containers and closures shall be provided adjacent to the potentisation section. This area shall have the following facilities, namely: - 
(i) washing tanks with suitable mechanical or hand operated brushes, bottle washing machines 
(rotary/linear) air-jet machine; 
(ii) rinsing tanks. Purified water shall be used for rinsing; 
(iii) closures washing/macerating tanks; 
 
(iv) driers. 
Note 1:- Different droppers shall be used only for each different medicine and different potency. 
Note 2:- All measures shall be in metric system. Measures used shall be of neutral glass or stainless steel. Metal droppers and plastic droppers shall not be used.
