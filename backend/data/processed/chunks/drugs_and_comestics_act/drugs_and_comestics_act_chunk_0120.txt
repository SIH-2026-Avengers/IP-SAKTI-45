Whoever himself or by any other person on his behalf manufactures for sale or for distribution, or sells, or stocks or exhibits or offers for sale- 
(i) any cosmetic deemed to be spurious under section 17D shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three years and with fine; 
(ii) any cosmetic other than a cosmetic referred to in clause (i) above in contravention of any provision of this Chapter or any rule made thereunder shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to one year or with fine which may extend to one thousand rupees or with both.]
