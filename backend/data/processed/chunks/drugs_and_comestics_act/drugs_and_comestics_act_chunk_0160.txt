33G. Inspectors. -(1) The Central Government or a State Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint such persons as it thinks fit, having the prescribed qualification, to be Inspectors for such areas as may be assigned to them by Central 
Government or the State Government as the case may be. 
 
(2) The powers which may be exercised by an Inspector and the duties which may be performed by him and the conditions, limitations or restrictions subject to which such powers and duties may be exercised or performed shall be such as may be prescribed.
