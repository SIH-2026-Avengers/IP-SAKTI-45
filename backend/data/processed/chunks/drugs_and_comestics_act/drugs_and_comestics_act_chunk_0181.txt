Certain offences to be tried summarily. -Notwithstanding anything contained in the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, all offences under this Act, punishable with imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years, other than an offence under clause (b) of sub-section (1) of section 33I, shall be tried in a summary way by a Judicial Magistrate of the first class specially empowered in this behalf by the State Government or by a Metropolitan 
Magistrate and the provisions of section 262 to265 (both inclusive) of the said Code shall, as far as may be, apply to such trial:
