33F. Government Analysts. -(1) The Central Government or a State Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint such person as it thinks fit, having the prescribed qualification, to be Government Analysts for such areas as may be assigned to them by the Central Government or the State Government ,as the case may be.
