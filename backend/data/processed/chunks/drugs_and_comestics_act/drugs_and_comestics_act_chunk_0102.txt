23. Procedure of Inspectors. -(1) Where an Inspector takes any sample of a drug 2[or cosmetic] under this Chapter, he shall tender the fair price thereof and may require a written acknowledgement therefor. 
 
(2) Where the price tendered under sub-section (1) is refused, or where the Inspector seizes the stock of any drug 2[or cosmetic] under clause (c) of section 22, he shall tender a receipt therefore in the prescribed form.
