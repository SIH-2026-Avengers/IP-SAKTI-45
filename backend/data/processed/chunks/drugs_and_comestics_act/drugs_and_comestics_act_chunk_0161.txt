33H. Application of provisions of section 22, 23, 24 and 25. -The provisions of section 
22, 23, 24 and 25 and the rules, if any, made thereunder shall, so far as may be, apply in relation to an Inspector and a Government Analyst appointed under this Chapter as they apply in relation to an Inspector and a Government Analyst appointed under Chapter IV, subject to the modification that the references to "drug" in the said section, shall be constructed as references to "Ayurvedic, 1[Siddha] or Unani Drugs."
