1[33D. The Ayurvedic, Siddha and Unani Drugs Consultative Committee. -(1) The 
Central Government may constitute an Advisory Committee to be called the Ayurvedic, 
Siddha and Unani Drugs Consultative Committee to advise the Central Government, the State 
Governments and the Ayurvedic, Siddha and Unani Drugs Technical Advisory Board on any matter for the purpose of securing uniformity throughout India in the administration of this 
Act in so far as it relates to Ayurvedic, Siddha or Unani drugs.
