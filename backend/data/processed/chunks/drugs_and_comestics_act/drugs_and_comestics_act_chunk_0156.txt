33EEB. Regulation of manufacture for sale of Ayurvedic, Siddha and Unani drugs. -No person shall manufacture for sale or for distribution any Ayurvedic, Siddha or Unani drug except in accordance with such standards, if any, as may be prescribed in relation to that drug.

(a) manufacture for sale or for distribution-- 
(i) any misbranded, adulterated or spurious Ayurvedic, Siddha or Unani drugs;
