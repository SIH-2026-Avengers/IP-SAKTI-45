7. The Drugs Consultative Committee. -(1) The Central Government may constitute an advisory committee to be called "the Drugs Consultative Committee" to advise the Central 
Government, the State Governments and the Drugs Technical Advisory Board on any other matter tending to secure uniformity throughout 4[India] in the administration of this Act. 
 
(2) The Drugs Consultative Committee shall consist of two representatives of the Central 
Government to be nominated by that Government and one representative of each State 
Government to be nominated by the State Government concerned.
