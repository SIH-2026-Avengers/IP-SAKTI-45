Ans.: Functions played by Ministry of Finance, MCA and Ministry of Law and Justice 
(A) Ministry of Finance: 
• The Ministry of Finance (Vitta Mantralaya) is a Ministry within the Government of India concerned with the economy of India, serving as the Treasury of India. 
• In particular, it concerns itself with taxation, financial legislation, financial institutions, capital markets, centre and state finances, and the Union Budget.
