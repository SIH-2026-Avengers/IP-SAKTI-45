Indian Regulatory Framework 
 
Chapter 1 
1.6 
• Regulation of interpretation of the Acts and Codes, 
• Dispute Resolution, and 
• Promotion of fairness among the citizens of the land. 
Indian Judicial System performs his functions through the hierarchy of courts, Supreme Court is at the top, followed by High Courts, District Courts and Metropolitan Courts. Decisions of a High Court are binding in the respective state but are only persuasive in other states. Decisions of Supreme Court are binding on all High Courts under Article 141 of the Indian Constitution. 
Supreme Court decision is the final word on the matter.
