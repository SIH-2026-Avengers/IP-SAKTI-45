1.3 - Indian Judicial System 
Q.9 
What is the significance of the Supreme Court and High Court in the Indian judiciary? 
[MTP-May 24, Aug. 25; RTP-Jan. 25] 
Ans.: Significance of the Supreme Court and High Court: 
(i) 
Supreme Court: Supreme Court is the apex body of judiciary. It was established on 26th January 
1950. Chief Justice of India (CJI) is the highest authority appointed under Article 126. The principal bench of the Supreme Court consists of 7 members including CJI. Presently, the number has increased to 34 including CJI due to rise in the number of cases and workload.
