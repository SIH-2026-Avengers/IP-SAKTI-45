How does it manage currency, payment systems and economic development? [RTP-Jan. 26] 
Ans.: Regulatory bodies of the Government of India: 
(i) The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI): 
• It is regulatory body for securities & commodity market in India under Ministry of Finance, 
Government of India. 
• It was established in 1988 as an executive body and was given statutory powers through the 
SEBI Act, 1992. 
(ii) Reserve Bank of India (RBI): 
• It is India's Central Bank and regulatory body responsible for regulation of the Indian banking system. 
• It is under the ownership of Ministry of Finance, Government of India. 
• It is responsible for the control, issue and maintaining supply of the Indian rupee.
