Ans.: Responsibilities and organizational structure of the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA): 
(a) MCA is an Indian Government Ministry. 
(b) It is primarily concerned with administration of the Companies Act 2013, the Companies Act 1956, the LLP Act, 2008, and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016. 
(c) It is responsible mainly for the regulation of Indian enterprises in the industrial and services sector. 
(d) The Ministry is mostly run by civil servants of the ICLS cadre. 
(e) These officers are elected through the Civil Services Examination conducted by Union Public 
Service Commission. 
(f) The highest post, Director General of Corporate Affairs (DGCoA), is fixed at Apex Scale for the ICLS.
