(iii) Common Law: A judicial precedent or a case law is common law. A judgment delivered by the 
Supreme Court will be binding upon the courts within the territory of India under Article 141 of the Indian Constitution. 
The doctrine of Stare Decisis is the principle supporting common law. It is a Latin phrase that means "to stand by that which is decided." The doctrine of Stare Decisis reinforces the obligation of courts to follow the same principle or judgement established by previous decisions. 
(iv) Principles of Natural Justice: Natural justice, often known as Jus Natural deals with certain fundamental principles of justice going beyond written law. 
A judgement can override or alter a common law, but it cannot override or change the statute.
