23.The functions of the State Biodiversity Board shall be to:- 
(a)advise the State Government, subject to any guidelines issued by the Central Government, on matters relating to the conservation of biodiversity, sustainable use of its components and equitable sharing of the benefits arising out of the utilization of biological resources; 
(b)regulate by granting of approvals or otherwise requests for commercial utilization or bio-survey and bio-utilisation of any biological resource by Indians; 
(c)perform such other functions as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act or as may be prescribed by the 
State Government. 
 
24.(1) Any citizen of India or a body corporate, organization or association registered in India intending to undertake any activity referred to in section 7 shall give prior intimation is such form as may be prescribed by the State Government to the 
State Biodiversity Board.
