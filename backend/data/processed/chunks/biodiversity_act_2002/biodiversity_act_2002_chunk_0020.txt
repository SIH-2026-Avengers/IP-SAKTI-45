13.(1) The National Biodiversity Authority may constitute a committee to deal with agro-biodiversity. 
Explanation - For the purposes of this sub-section, "agro-biodiversity" means biological diversity of agriculture related species and their wild relatives. 
(2)Without prejudice to the provisions of sub-section (1), the National Biodiversity Authority may constitute such number of committees as it deems fit for the efficient discharge of its duties and performance of its function under this Act. 
(3)A committee constituted under this section shall-co-opt such number of persons, who are not the members of the 
National Biodiversity Authority, as it may think fit and the persons so co-opted shall have the right to attend the meetings of the committee and take part in its proceedings but shall not have the right to vote.
